**Date:** 21 November 2026  
**Market:** Pakistan

## Strategy summary

Module 03 supplies 150 candidate keywords across informational, commercial investigation, transactional and navigational intent. It includes 38 seeds and 110 long-tail candidates, but no reliable search-volume, organic difficulty, or CPC data. Module 02 records visible competitor category/product taxonomy for Paklap, Supreme Traders, Overstock-PK, Czone, Shophive and Daraz; it does not provide a comprehensive content crawl or verified rankings. Accordingly, the content gaps in this module are opportunity hypotheses, not verified competitor omissions.

The strategy groups work into ten clusters: laptop buying guides, student laptops, gaming laptops, HP, Dell, Lenovo, comparisons, accessories, maintenance and troubleshooting. Build a useful pillar guide and practical supporting articles, then connect them to accurate, populated category and product pages. Only publish commercial pages when the product catalog supports them. Do not build thin price, city, filter or brand pages merely to match query variations.

## Three-month plan

The proposed editorial calendar contains 12 weekly assignments from 1 December 2026 through 16 February 2027. It mixes buying advice, student/gaming selection, comparison, compatibility, troubleshooting and price-comparison topics. Word-count ranges are planning estimates tied to the decision scope; writers should stop when the reader's questions are answered, not pad to a target. Several pieces depend on TechFind confirming used/refurbished inventory, accessories, warranty terms and product compatibility.

Ten detailed briefs are supplied, plus three complete article drafts:

1. Laptop buying guide: how to choose for your needs.
2. Student laptop checklist.
3. How to compare laptop specifications.

The articles use durable decision frameworks and avoid made-up prices, models, stock, reviews and performance claims. Verify and refresh linked software/manufacturer documentation before publication. The available web search service failed during this module, so external sources were not independently opened.
