# Content Distribution Strategy

**Status:** Proposed organic distribution plan. No TechFind social accounts, email list, customer audience or publishing operations were supplied.

## Principles

- Publish and verify the canonical article first; distribution should help real readers discover it.
- Adapt each piece to the platform rather than pasting identical promotional copy everywhere.
- Be transparent about TechFind's relationship to a product or business. Follow platform rules and community guidelines.
- Do not scrape contacts, spam groups, buy engagement, or post repetitive links in unrelated communities.
- Use UTM campaign tags consistently for links, while retaining the canonical page URL.

## Channel plan

| Channel | Useful format | Audience approach | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Short checklist carousel, practical excerpt, Q&A post | Share in an owned page/community; join relevant groups only where self-promotion is allowed and the post answers a real question | Referral sessions, engaged sessions, product/category clicks |
| LinkedIn | Professional buying framework, procurement/student technology considerations, concise article summary | Use a company/person account with clear authorship; contribute expertise without turning every post into a sales pitch | Qualified referral sessions, saves/comments, assisted actions where measurable |
| Pinterest | Vertical checklist graphic or comparison worksheet linking to canonical guide | Use descriptive pin titles and accessible image text; link to the useful source page | Outbound clicks and engaged landing sessions |
| Technology communities | Answer a specific question and cite the relevant guide only if it adds useful detail | Read rules first; disclose affiliation; participate consistently and accept moderation | Helpful responses, referral quality; avoid raw link-volume targets |
| Email newsletter (if list exists) | Short monthly digest, new guide and one actionable checklist | Send only to opted-in subscribers; segment by interests; honor preferences and unsubscribe requests | Delivery, clicks, engaged sessions, attributed conversions; monitor unsubscribes/complaints |

## Suggested launch workflow

1. Confirm the piece is published, accurate, mobile-readable and linked from relevant site pages.
2. Create one platform-specific excerpt and visual; make claims traceable to the page's source notes.
3. Add consistent UTM tags and record post date/channel/content URL in an editorial log.
4. Respond to genuine questions; correct or update the source article if feedback reveals a factual gap.
5. Review qualified referral activity monthly. Do not equate likes or impressions with sales or SEO impact.

No social posting or email distribution has been performed. Account access and permission are not assumed.
