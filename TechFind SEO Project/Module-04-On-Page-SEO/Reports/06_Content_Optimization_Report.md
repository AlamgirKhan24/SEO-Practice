# Content Optimization Report

**Date:** 21 November 2026  
**Scope:** proposed TechFind architecture and Module 03 mapping. There is no supplied live URL, catalog, or source copy, so this is an editorial specification rather than an audit of existing content.

## Content recommendations

- **Homepage:** clarify the real seller identity and route shoppers to available laptop categories, accessories and guides. Add trust/service details only after the owner verifies them. Avoid generic claims such as “best prices” or nationwide delivery unless substantiated.
- **Laptop and brand categories:** provide concise, distinct selection help and usable filters. Explain what shoppers can compare. Do not publish empty category copy or repeat one template paragraph across HP, Dell and Lenovo.
- **Gaming/student categories:** use actual specifications and selection criteria. Do not assert performance, student suitability, or budget fit without evidence.
- **Product pages:** write around the exact configuration. Verify every specification, price, stock state, package item, warranty and delivery statement from authoritative records. See `Templates/09_Product_Page_SEO_Template.md`.
- **Buying guides:** answer a defined user question with accurate, original guidance. Explain processors, memory, storage and display in context; avoid unsupported rankings or advice that assumes one configuration fits every user.
- **Comparison pages:** compare named, current configurations on like-for-like attributes and document the source/date of facts. Explain trade-offs and avoid brand-wide winners.
- **About, contact and policy pages:** use verified company identity, active contact channels, approved policy wording and accurate hours. Operational/legal review is needed for returns, warranty, payment and privacy claims.

## Readability and UX

Put the decision-critical facts near the top, use short paragraphs and descriptive section headings, present specifications in accessible tables, and make filters and purchase controls work with keyboard and touch. Images need useful alternatives and stable dimensions. Keep text readable on small screens and do not hide essential information behind inaccessible controls. Use clear calls to action that match actual stock and checkout state.

## Keyword use and duplication

Use the single primary topic in the title, H1 and naturally in relevant copy. Supporting phrases should appear only where useful. Keep one primary page per intent from Module 03; budget variations, filters, and product variants need a deliberate URL/canonical strategy. Do not create thin city or price pages without distinct service and catalog value.

## Status

No existing text was available to rewrite, and no content has been deployed. Draft title/description/headings and page frameworks are in the `Optimization/` CSVs and `Templates/` folder. All recommendations remain conditional on actual inventory, business facts and live-site review.
