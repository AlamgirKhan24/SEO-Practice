# Off-Page SEO Implementation Guidelines

## Prospect qualification

Before outreach, confirm: (1) the domain and destination page are live and accurate; (2) the prospect page exists and is relevant to the intended audience; (3) the publisher has a real editorial or resource function; (4) public contact/submission information is current; and (5) the proposed asset directly benefits its readers. Record evidence and review date. Do not use domain authority alone as a quality filter.

## Ethical execution

- Earn citations through useful content, expertise and genuine relationships.
- Do not pay for followed ranking links, use PBNs/link farms, automate bulk link creation, spam forums/comments, or demand exact-match anchors.
- For sponsorships, affiliate relationships, samples and other compensated placements, disclose clearly and apply appropriate link qualification under current search-engine and publisher policies.
- Make no claims about TechFind's expertise, ownership, customer outcomes, testing or partnerships without evidence.
- Send outreach only to relevant public business contacts. Personalize messages, limit follow-up, honor opt-outs, and follow applicable privacy/anti-spam rules.

## Recording and verification

Use the tracker fields for prospect, contact method, target page, outreach/follow-up dates, status and notes. After a link or mention appears, manually verify the referring URL, target, context, anchor, link attribute, status and date. Keep screenshots or exports with source/date where available. A provider's first-seen date is an estimate, not necessarily the placement date.

Suggested response statuses: Not contacted; Researching; Ready for review; Sent; Follow-up due; Replied; Declined; No response/closed; Opted out. Link statuses: Not applicable; Not placed; Pending; Live editorial; Live sponsored/qualified; Removed; Broken; Needs review. Do not label a prospect as contacted or a link as earned until evidence exists.

## Monitoring and disavow safeguards

Review Search Console link data and optional provider exports periodically, manually checking material changes. Relevance, context and editorial legitimacy matter more than a single third-party toxicity number. Do not submit a disavow file solely because a tool flags a domain; investigate evidence of manipulative links and consult current Search Console documentation and qualified advice.

## Measurement

Report new/lost links, relevant referring domains, editorially earned links, anchors, referral visits/engagement, conversions and brand mentions with a measurement source and date. Separate measured observations from estimates and proposed targets. Do not promise ranking gains or attribute organic changes to links without a careful analysis of other factors.
