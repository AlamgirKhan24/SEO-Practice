# Linkable Asset Strategy

All assets below are proposed, based on Module 06 content planning. None is published and no original research or statistics have been conducted.

| Asset | Audience and link potential | Prospective publishers/audience | Outreach angle and promotion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laptop buying guide | General buyers; useful reference for selecting by workload and verified specs | Technology publications, student guidance pages, consumer resources | Offer as a practical checklist; promote through owned social and relevant communities |
| Student laptop checklist | Students, parents, university support pages | University libraries/student services where resource submission is appropriate; education publications | Share a neutral checklist aligned to software requirements; no university endorsement implied |
| Gaming laptop comparison framework | Gaming readers comparing components and configuration | Gaming/technology publications and communities | Explain how to compare exact GPU/configuration and conditions; no blanket product rankings |
| Specific model comparison | Buyers of matched current configurations | Review publications and technology writers | Pitch a factual comparison with sources, methodology, availability date and limitations |
| Laptop maintenance and safe troubleshooting guides | Device owners seeking safe first steps | Repair education resources, user communities, technology blogs | Offer manufacturer-referenced safe advice and clear escalation boundaries |
| Accessory compatibility reference | Laptop owners choosing compatible chargers/docks/accessories | Tech help resources and product-focused publications | Provide compatibility checks by exact model where evidence exists; do not claim universal fit |
| Configuration comparison worksheet | Shoppers comparing exact listings | Consumer/student resources and technology communities | Offer a downloadable neutral worksheet with no invented performance scores |
| Original Pakistan laptop price/availability snapshot (planned research) | Buyers and press interested in market observations | Technology journalists, consumer publications, research/education pages | Conduct a transparent, repeatable sample with date, scope, source, limitations and no unsupported national extrapolation. This research has not been conducted. |

## Production safeguards

- Confirm actual domain, catalog and business identity before creating assets that refer to TechFind as a live retailer.
- Source technical facts from manufacturers/software providers and record source/date.
- If conducting original research, predefine sampling, collection method, date, inclusion criteria and limitations; retain raw data. Label proposed research as planned until completed.
- Do not publish unverifiable rankings, review scores, market statistics or availability claims.
- Ensure every asset is accessible, stable, useful without a link, and internally linked from the relevant category/page.

## Promotion channels

Use approved TechFind social accounts, opt-in email, relevant communities under their rules, and individually researched editorial pitches. Refer to `Reports/11_Off_Page_SEO_Implementation_Guidelines.md` for qualification and monitoring. No outreach has occurred.
