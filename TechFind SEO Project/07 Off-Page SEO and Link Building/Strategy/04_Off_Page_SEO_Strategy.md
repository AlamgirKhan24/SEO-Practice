## White-hat approaches

| Approach | Execution and benefit | Resources and risks |
|---|---|---|
| Guest contributions | Pitch a genuinely useful, original article to a publication that serves laptop buyers or technology readers. Let the editor decide attribution and link placement. | Subject expert, writer, editorial research. Avoid scaled guest-post exchanges, thin content and demanded exact-match anchors. |
| Digital PR | Offer a journalist a useful expert comment, product-context explanation or transparent original research after it exists. | Responsive subject expert, verified evidence, media contact research. Never manufacture findings, credentials or news hooks. |
| Resource pages | Find maintained educational/technology resource pages where a specific TechFind guide fills a genuine user need. | Manual page check and relevant asset. A generic link request is unlikely to help readers; no paid placement for ranking purposes. |
| Broken links | Confirm a relevant resource is actually broken and propose a live page that serves the same need. | Manual re-test and suitable replacement. Do not send automated bulk reports or claim a link is broken without evidence. |
| Unlinked mentions | After TechFind has brand mentions, locate public references and politely request a link only where attribution benefits readers. | Brand/domain monitoring and proof of mention. Not actionable before the brand/domain is active. |
| Supplier/partner references | Ask legitimate suppliers, schools, organizations or business partners to list TechFind only where the relationship and page genuinely warrant it. | Confirm real relationship and accuracy. Never create fake memberships or require followed links. |
| Product reviews | Provide review samples only under transparent editorial arrangements, with no control over coverage or required positive/followed links. | Disclosure, sample logistics and policies. Paid/sponsored placements must be clearly disclosed and use appropriate link qualification; never buy ranking links. |
| Expert contributions | Make a qualified expert available for accurate technical comments or Q&A. | Verifiable expertise, response process, reviewed claims. Do not invent credentials or experience. |
| Community participation | Answer real questions in relevant technology communities and link only when allowed and useful. | Community rule review and ongoing participation. No spam comments, forum drops or promotional flooding. |

## Prioritization and qualification

First qualify the destination and asset: the target page must be live, useful, accurate, stable and appropriate for the audience. Then review each prospect's actual page and public contact method. Priority in the tracker is qualitative: high means strong audience relevance and a clear editorial value; medium means plausible but conditional; low means weak or unverified. No authority score or traffic estimate is used.

## Anchor text

Prefer natural editorial language chosen by the publisher. The anchor strategy CSV contains examples of branded, naked URL, generic, descriptive, partial-match and cautious exact-match anchors. Do not prescribe a ratio or demand exact-match anchors. Avoid repeated keyword-rich anchors, reciprocal schemes, sitewide widgets and wording that misrepresents a destination.

## Outreach workflow

1. Confirm TechFind's domain, business identity and live target page.
2. Verify the prospect's page and public submission/contact route manually.
3. Find one specific editorial fit and explain reader benefit.
4. Make one concise, factual, personalized pitch; never imply prior contact or a relationship that does not exist.
5. Follow up once only where appropriate; honor no-response and opt-out signals.
6. Record message date/status in the tracker. Verify any resulting link manually and record the referring URL, target, anchor, rel attribute and date.
7. Report qualified editorial outcomes and referral engagement, not just counts or domain metrics.

No messages have been sent. Email templates are drafts in `Outreach/06_Link_Building_Outreach_Templates.md`.

## 90-day plan and measurement

The 90-day plan is in `Strategy/08_90_Day_Link_Building_Plan.csv`. Days 1–30 establish the domain, baseline, target assets and vetted prospects. Days 31–60 begin small, personalized, value-led campaigns. Days 61–90 use respectful follow-up, verify earned mentions/links and review referral/analytics data. It sets no link or ranking quota.
