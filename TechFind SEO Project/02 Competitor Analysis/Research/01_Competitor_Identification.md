# Competitor Identification — Pakistan Laptop Ecommerce

**Observed:** 21 November 2026 | **Selection basis:** relevance to laptop shopping in Pakistan and direct observation of public pages.

|---|---|---|---|

**Why these are not a definitive ranking:** The set combines specialized sellers, broader retailers, and a marketplace. It was assembled from a single general Google query plus direct site observations, not from paid market-share data or a complete laptop SERP set. Use it as a practical first benchmark set, then refine in Module 03/04 using the actual TechFind categories and query set.

## Excluded or unconfirmed candidate

Google surfaced an IT Zone Electronics laptop-category result at `https://itzoneelectronics.com/product-category/laptop/`, but opening it showed a Hostinger “Your domain is expired” page. It is therefore not counted as a currently observable competitor. PCStore appeared in the same result set but displayed a browser verification challenge; it is noted as an unverified candidate, not analyzed. These observations are date-specific and should be rechecked before future use.

## Classification notes

- **Direct** means product overlap with a proposed laptop retailer; it does not imply identical business model, scale, or service area.
- **Indirect** means broader electronics/computer assortment competes for some laptop/product discovery and purchase intent.
- **Marketplace** means many sellers offer overlapping goods on a platform that competes for category and model searches while also hosting seller listings.
- None of these sites' search rankings, traffic, sales, market share, backlink counts, or conversion rates have been measured.
