2. Publish and verify useful, stable assets from Module 06 before prospecting.
3. Manually qualify relevant technology, education and editorial prospects; record public contact method and evidence. No contact data has been verified yet.
4. Prioritize editorial value, expertise, genuine partnerships and appropriate disclosure; reject link schemes, paid ranking links, spam and guaranteed-link offers.
5. Track actual earned links and referral behavior; do not set a backlink quota or claim ranking impact in advance.
