## Sending rules

- Personalize based on actual page review; don't use scraped bulk lists.
- Keep a suppression/opt-out record and stop on request.
- Avoid deceptive subject lines, fake familiarity, guaranteed claims and link quotas.
- Do not offer payment or benefits in exchange for ranking links. Disclose legitimate sponsorships and follow applicable publisher rules.
- No outreach will be sent until the user explicitly approves execution.
