# Local Review and Reputation Templates

**Use only after TechFind has eligible real customer transactions and the actual channel is confirmed.** No request or response has been sent.

## Neutral review request

**Subject:** Tell us about your TechFind experience

Hello [customer name],

Thank you for choosing [verified business name]. If you have a moment, you can share an honest review of your experience here: [official review link]. Your feedback helps us understand what worked and what we can improve. A review is entirely optional, and your support or any benefit is not tied to posting one.

Regards,  
[real team/contact]

Send consistently to eligible customers; do not filter recipients based on satisfaction.

## Positive public response

Thank you for sharing your experience, [name]. We’re glad [specific point mentioned, if appropriate] was helpful. We appreciate your feedback.

## Negative public response

Thank you for bringing this to our attention. We’re sorry to hear that your experience did not meet expectations. We’d like to understand and review the issue. Please contact us at [verified private support channel] with [minimal reference needed]; please don’t post personal or order information publicly.

Avoid arguing, revealing customer data, making promises before investigation, or copying a generic response that ignores the concern. Follow up privately and resolve where possible.

## Internal feedback triage

Record review date, channel, issue category, responsible owner, private follow-up status and resolution. Restrict personal/order details to approved systems and staff. Escalate safety, privacy, legal, fraud or threat concerns through established processes. Never pressure a reviewer to change or remove a review in exchange for resolution.
