# Local SEO Implementation Guidelines

## Before publishing local signals

- Verify the business model and Google Business Profile eligibility from actual operations.
- Verify legal/real-world business name, customer-facing address, phone, staffed hours, website, categories and service coverage against primary records.
- Do not use virtual offices, mailbox addresses, employee homes or keyword-stuffed names as storefronts. Do not invent a city branch or service area.
- Search for duplicates and claim the existing legitimate listing where possible.

## Before creating a city page

Require a real location or genuinely distinct local service/offer, unique local customer information, an operational owner, and a clear reason the page helps a customer. Verify availability, collection/pickup, pricing, contact, hours, delivery and service claims. If the only change is city name, do not publish.

## Citations and reputation

Use a small number of relevant, trustworthy listings. Check listing terms, duplicate state, verification and paid-upgrade conditions. Keep NAP facts consistent only for locations the business actually operates. Never buy fake reviews, gate review requests, offer review incentives or expose customer details in replies.

## Tracking and review

Keep source, access date, scope and methodology beside every metric. Use Search Console for query/page/country data, GA4 for sessions/events/conversions after implementation, and Business Profile performance only for eligible verified profiles. Avoid treating clicks/directions as completed visits or attributed sales without corroborating data.
