# TechFind — E-commerce SEO project

**Project status:** Portfolio research and planning documented; live validation and implementation pending  
**Market:** Pakistan  
**Business concept:** Online store for laptops and technology products  
**Project type:** Professional portfolio SEO project

## Project purpose

Plan and document a practical SEO program for TechFind to improve organic discoverability, attract relevant searchers, and support qualified product discovery and sales. This is a project plan, not evidence that a live website has been audited or improved. No rankings, traffic, conversions, or backlink results have been measured or claimed.

2. Competitor Analysis
3. Keyword Research & Strategy
4. On-Page SEO Optimization
5. Technical SEO Audit & Optimization
6. Content SEO Strategy
7. Off-Page SEO & Link Building
8. Local SEO (optional; applicability to be decided)
9. SEO Monitoring & Analytics
10. SEO Reporting & Portfolio Documentation

The project is organized into ten modules, with research and planning artifacts documented in the corresponding folders. Any live-site validation, implementation, or public-facing claims remain pending until the business facts, access, and approvals are confirmed. Each module records its purpose, methods, deliverables, verification status, and limitations.

## Business concept and audience

TechFind is planned as an online retailer serving Pakistan with laptop and technology product discovery and purchase. Priority audience groups provided for this project are students and university students, professionals, freelancers, gamers, and technology enthusiasts. Likely customer needs include comparing specifications and prices, finding products for a particular use or budget, checking availability and delivery/returns information, and assessing seller trust. These are planning hypotheses to validate during business research, not measured customer insights.

## Proposed site structure

Subject to the real catalog, fulfillment model, and site platform:

- Home
- Laptops
  - By use: student, business, gaming, creator (only where catalog supports useful landing pages)
  - By brand (only where inventory and demand justify it)
  - Product detail pages
- Desktops / components / accessories / other technology categories (confirm actual assortment before creating)
- Buying guides and comparison resources
- About TechFind and contact
- Delivery and shipping, payment methods, returns/refunds, warranty, and customer support policies
- Account, cart, and checkout

Indexation, faceting, filters, pagination, and URL rules must be designed around the actual platform and inventory. Avoid creating thin or duplicative category pages. Legal and policy page requirements will be confirmed with the business; this document is not legal advice.

## Required inputs and access

Before research or implementation, obtain or confirm:

- Official domain and preferred canonical hostname; staging/test URLs if applicable.
- Whether TechFind is an operating business, a concept, or a portfolio-only mock project; identify which facts can be represented publicly.

- Permission and technical contact for any requested website changes, plus backup/release process.

Do not share passwords, API secrets, customer personal data, or payment data in the project documents. Use delegated account permissions and redacted exports.

The proposed site structure and audience needs remain hypotheses, and KPI baselines are unavailable.
