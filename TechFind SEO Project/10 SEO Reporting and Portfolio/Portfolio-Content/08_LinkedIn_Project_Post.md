# LinkedIn Project Post Draft

I completed a portfolio SEO strategy project for **TechFind**, a proposed laptop and technology ecommerce concept targeting Pakistan.

The work covers the full planning lifecycle: competitor desk research, keyword intent and page mapping, on-page recommendations, technical SEO readiness, content clusters and draft articles, ethical link-building, local eligibility, and an analytics framework.

One important constraint shaped the work: there was no confirmed live domain, catalog, Search Console/GA4 data, or backlink profile. I kept volumes, rankings, traffic, and outcomes marked unavailable instead of filling the gaps with estimates. Every page, content and outreach recommendation is labeled as proposed until business facts can be verified.

This is a strategy and documentation portfolio project—not a live client implementation or performance case study. I’m proud of the evidence discipline and the practical handoff structure, and I’m looking forward to applying the framework to a live ecommerce site.
