# Portfolio Website Description

## TechFind — E-commerce SEO Strategy for a Proposed Pakistan Laptop Retailer

Designed an end-to-end SEO research and launch strategy for a proposed laptop and technology ecommerce concept serving Pakistan. The project includes qualitative competitor analysis, 150 source-labeled keyword candidates, intent clusters and proposed page mapping, on-page specifications, technical SEO readiness checks, a content plan with three draft articles, ethical link-building and conditional local SEO strategies, and a GSC/GA4 measurement framework.

**Scope:** Research and documentation only. TechFind had no confirmed live domain, catalog, analytics properties or backlink profile. No site changes, outreach, rankings, traffic, sales or backlinks are claimed. Spreadsheet deliverables are CSV fallbacks where XLSX export failed.

**Skills demonstrated:** evidence qualification, ecommerce information architecture, keyword intent mapping, editorial planning, technical SEO readiness, ethical outreach planning, analytics design, and professional documentation.
