# TechFind — E-commerce SEO Optimization

**Portfolio case study | Proposed project, not a live client engagement**

## Project overview

TechFind is a proposed Pakistan-focused ecommerce concept for laptops and technology products. The goal of this portfolio project was to create a practical SEO foundation across research, page planning, content, technical readiness, off-page strategy, local eligibility, and measurement.

## Business challenge

The project began without a confirmed business domain, platform, catalog, policies, location, or first-party analytics. Laptop ecommerce also has many similar configurations, dynamic pricing and stock, competitive category pages, and information needs across students, gaming and general buyers. The main challenge was to create useful strategy without presenting unverified assumptions as facts.

## Research and analysis

The planning module defined a proposed business and architecture, documenting missing inputs. Competitor desk research observed six Pakistani retailer/marketplace sites and compared visible taxonomy and product language; it did not measure rankings or backlink performance. Keyword work assembled 38 seeds, 150 candidate phrases and 110 long-tail ideas, with source labels distinguishing observed suggestions from expansions. Search volume, difficulty and CPC were left unavailable.

## SEO strategy

The strategy centers on one useful page per distinct intent: a laptop hub; brand/use-case categories only when actual stock supports them; exact product pages with verified configuration; buying and comparison guides; and accessories where compatibility data exists. Metadata, heading, image, internal-link, and page templates are prepared as recommendations. City pages and Google Business Profile are conditional on real eligibility and local value.

## Technical optimization

A technical readiness package defines checks for robots, sitemap, indexability, canonicals, facets, redirects, mobile, Core Web Vitals and structured data. Because there was no site to crawl, no technical condition or fix is claimed as observed or resolved.

## Content strategy

Ten topic clusters, 26 content opportunity hypotheses, a 12-week editorial calendar and ten briefs were created, alongside three sample article drafts. Drafts avoid fabricated specs/prices and require source and expert review before publication.

## Off-page SEO

A white-hat strategy, anchor guidance, outreach templates, asset ideas, KPI plan and 90-day roadmap were documented. The prospect sheet is blank because no prospect/contact could be independently verified in the evidence available. No outreach or link building occurred.

## Tools used

- Local project documents/CSVs for cross-module synthesis.
- Google Search in a browser for limited autocomplete and a single qualitative SERP sample (Module 03).
- Google Trends Explore was opened but produced no usable trend values.
- Web search for Pakistan local market and Google Business Profile research in Module 08.
- Python/PowerShell for local file organization and CSV table generation.

Not used: Google Keyword Planner exports, Ahrefs, Semrush, Search Console, GA4, Screaming Frog, Lighthouse, PageSpeed Insights, or live CMS/site access. Spreadsheet runtime import succeeded in later work, but XLSX exports repeatedly failed; CSV alternatives were delivered.

## Deliverables

Ten modules of research, strategy, page/content specifications, templates and monitoring frameworks. Tabular workbook requests are generally represented as CSV fallbacks; consult each module's fallback note. No deployment package or performance dashboard with real results exists.

## Results and learning

There are no verified rankings, traffic, conversions, sales, backlinks, or technical performance results. The project demonstrates evidence-aware planning: mark missing data clearly, sequence recommendations behind business facts, avoid unsupported claims, and define what must be instrumented before measuring SEO impact.

## Conclusion

The work provides a structured launch and measurement plan that can be converted into implementation after TechFind confirms its domain, catalog, operating model, and policies. A genuine results case study will require deployment and an agreed data baseline.
