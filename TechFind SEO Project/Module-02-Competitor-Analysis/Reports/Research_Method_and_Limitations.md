# Research Method and Evidence Limits

**Snapshot date:** 21 November 2026

## Method performed

1. One Google Search query: `Pakistan laptop store online buy laptops Pakistan`.
2. Browser inspection of Paklap and Daraz homepages; Supreme Traders HP laptop category; Czone and Shophive homepages; and one Overstock-PK laptop product URL.
3. Recorded visible navigation, category labels, product-card copy, help/policy links, trust claims, document titles, and selected URL patterns from those page snapshots.
4. Opened one IT Zone Electronics category URL and observed an expired-domain page; one PCStore URL presented a browser verification challenge.

## Not performed / unavailable

- No Ahrefs, Semrush, Similarweb, Google Keyword Planner, or Google Trends account/report was available. No traffic, link, difficulty, authority, or volume estimates are reported.
- The web search service failed at network transport during setup; a single Google query and browser-based page review succeeded. The search sample is therefore narrow and query-specific.
- No Screaming Frog crawl or crawlable URL export; no complete URL discovery, internal link count, response-code audit, duplicate analysis, or broken-link check.
- No PageSpeed Insights/CrUX measurement; no field Core Web Vitals or load time claim.
- No desktop/mobile viewport test or assistive-tech UX test. Responsive behavior is not rated.
- No inspection of robots.txt, XML sitemap, canonical tags, meta descriptions, image ALT values, schema markup, or source HTML. These fields are marked unverified in the audit CSVs, not assumed absent.
- No competitor Search Console/GA4 access; no rankings, revenue, sales, conversions or organic-traffic comparison.
- No backlink index, referring-domain counts, link quality, anchor text distribution, or toxicity analysis.
- Dynamic inventory, prices, delivery and policy claims are self-published page observations only. They may change and are not independently validated.

## Reproducibility guidance

Save dated page screenshots and crawl exports during any later formal audit. Record requested URL, final URL after redirects, viewport, date/time, tool/version, filters, and page-template sample. Recheck all sites before using this research in public portfolio material because ecommerce sites and inventory change frequently.
