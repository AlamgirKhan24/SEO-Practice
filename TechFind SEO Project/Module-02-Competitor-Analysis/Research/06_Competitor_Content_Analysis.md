## Content gaps and TechFind opportunities

The following are **content opportunity hypotheses**, not verified keyword gaps or claims that all competitors lack these subjects. Validate demand and competing URLs in Module 03 before prioritizing publication.

1. **Laptop buying by budget bands in PKR** — transparent trade-offs and what changes as budget rises; update the advice when stock/prices change.
2. **Student laptop selection by workload** — browser/office, programming, design, engineering software, portability and battery, with no blanket “best for every student” claim.
3. **Business and freelancer laptop selection** — video calls, multitasking, portability, ports, service and downtime considerations.
4. **Gaming laptop GPU tiers and real trade-offs** — GPU/VRAM, cooling, display refresh, noise, battery and wattage explained without unsupported benchmark claims.
5. **Creator laptop display checklist** — resolution, gamut, brightness, memory and workflow fit, with measurement sources and caveats.
6. **Exact model/variant decoder** — how to distinguish model suffixes, regional keyboard/display/configuration variants, and SKU differences.
7. **New, open-box, used, and refurbished condition guide** — define inspection, battery/parts status, cosmetic grade, provenance, warranty and return treatment before offering these states.
8. **Laptop warranty explained for Pakistan buyers** — seller versus manufacturer warranty, covered region, proof of purchase and claim process, only using approved facts.
9. **Laptop delivery, returns and payment guide** — explain actual coverage, timing, fees and supported payment methods in a clear pre-purchase checklist.
10. **Side-by-side comparisons of currently stocked models** — date-stamped facts, fair criteria and explicit stock/price checked date.
11. **Upgrade and compatibility guide** — RAM/SSD slots, maximum capacity, warranty implications and compatible accessories based on manufacturer documentation.
12. **Laptop care, battery and thermal maintenance** — safe, model-appropriate advice reviewed by a knowledgeable technician.

## Editorial quality controls

Every commercial comparison should name exact model IDs, state the date checked, distinguish sourced specifications from measured results, and disclose missing data. Assign an informed reviewer and review date; link advice to relevant in-stock products without disguising sales intent. Avoid templated location pages, unsupported “best/cheapest/official” claims, copied manufacturer text, and stale SKU roundups.
