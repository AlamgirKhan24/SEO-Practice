## Assessment

TechFind's real operating model and locations are unconfirmed. On evidence currently available, treat it as online-only and **do not create a Google Business Profile** unless the owner verifies a qualifying in-person storefront or direct in-person service operation. Google says online-only businesses without in-person customer contact are ineligible. No address, city, phone, hours, service area, profile or listing was fabricated or created.

Pakistan sources provide broad context: PBS HIES 2024–25 reports household laptop ownership and internet-access indicators nationally and by urban/rural areas, while Trade.gov provides national ecommerce context. Neither provides TechFind city-level query demand. The city list is a set of hypotheses, not validated demand or competition. Keyword volumes/difficulty are all unavailable.
