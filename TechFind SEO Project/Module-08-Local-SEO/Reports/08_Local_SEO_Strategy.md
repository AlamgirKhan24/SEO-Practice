**Research snapshot:** 21 November 2026  
**Business status:** TechFind is proposed; the project record does not confirm a customer-facing premises, in-person services, domain, NAP data, delivery coverage or local inventory.

## Eligibility decision

**Current status: eligibility is unconfirmed. Do not create a Google Business Profile yet.** Google says a business must make in-person contact with customers during its stated hours; profiles are not for online-only businesses. A genuine staffed storefront where customers can visit may qualify. A service-area business may qualify when it directly visits or serves customers in person at their locations; it must accurately represent its operating model and address visibility. Ordinary nationwide shipping from an online-only store does not, by itself, justify inventing storefronts or city profiles. Verify current policy and TechFind's actual operations before applying. [Google Business Profile eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7039811?hl=en), [business representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)

The current project has no evidence of a storefront, staffed hours, customer visits or in-person service. Therefore this plan assumes **online-only until the owner supplies contrary evidence**. No profile has been created and no address, phone, hours or service area has been invented.

## Location pages

**Recommendation now: no city landing pages.** No unique city service, store, local assortment, customer support team, pickup option, or verified delivery promise has been supplied. Replacing city names in duplicated pages risks doorway-like content and misleads customers.

The CSV decision sheet gives two conditional templates: (1) a genuine customer-facing storefront page and (2) a real in-person service-area page. Each must contain unique, verified information such as location access, staffed hours, actual services, local inventory, photos, contact and coverage. Delivery-only availability should normally be presented on a national delivery page, not cloned into dozens of city pages.

If an eligible store exists, align the page with the actual store name and address. Use accurate structured data only for real facts, consistent with the visible page. Do not mark a home or virtual office as a storefront. Avoid duplicating a city page for each product brand/category unless each is independently useful.

## Google Business Profile plan (conditional)

Only proceed after eligibility is verified:

1. Confirm a real storefront customers can visit during stated staffed hours, or a qualifying business that directly visits/serves customers in person. Keep evidence of legal name, signage, location/operations, and authorization to represent the business.
2. Search for existing profiles and duplicates before creating anything. Use the real-world business name without keyword additions.
3. Select the closest accurate primary category and only relevant secondary categories from the live category picker; do not guess or stuff categories.
4. Enter the genuine business phone and official website. Set staffed hours accurately and keep holiday hours current.
5. Storefront businesses should represent the actual customer-facing location. A qualifying service-area business that does not receive customers at its base should follow Google's address-hiding rules and declare only areas it actually serves. Google describes service areas by city/postal code/area and limits the number; check current rules at setup. [Service areas guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481?hl=en)
6. Add actual product/service information and original current photos. Do not use stock images as proof of premises or publish products/services not offered.
7. Verify the profile using Google's offered method; retain owner access and monitor edits/duplicates.
8. Request honest reviews from all eligible customers without incentives or gating; answer professionally and protect customer privacy.

Until verified, no business description, categories, address, hours, phone, service area or photos should be drafted as if factual.

## Citation and NAP strategy

NAP means the same business **Name, Address and Phone**. A pure online business should not create fake local NAP citations. If a genuine location is confirmed, keep its official name/address/phone/hours consistent on the website, eligible profile, high-quality directories and legitimate association listings. Distinguish headquarters from service coverage and ensure any address is customer-facing only where required/allowed.

The citation opportunities CSV includes official research sources, potentially useful Pakistan directories (Ypages.pk and PakistanYP), and legitimate chamber options contingent on actual eligibility/membership. Directory presence was verified at the site level only; the quality, search indexation, verification demands, fees and listing performance were not exhaustively audited. Review terms and existing records before submission. Prefer accurate, relevant sources; skip low-quality paid upsells, duplicate submissions or any request to misrepresent location.

## Reviews and reputation

Request reviews only after genuine customer experiences, with a neutral request sent consistently. Do not offer incentives, ask only happy customers, write reviews, or condition support on a review. The `Reviews/05_Review_Management_Plan.csv` outlines controls. Templates for request and response are in the implementation guidelines. Reply without exposing order details; take service resolution to a private channel.
