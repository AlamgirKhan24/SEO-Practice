# TechFind SEO Measurement Framework

**Status:** Proposed measurement design; no live site, Search Console property, GA4 property, ranking export, crawl baseline or revenue data was supplied.

## Objectives

**Business objective:** generate qualified laptop/accessory purchases or enquiries in Pakistan, once the actual business and commerce model are confirmed.  
**SEO objectives:** increase visibility for relevant Pakistan queries; acquire qualified organic visits to useful category, product and guide pages; support product discovery and purchase; maintain crawl/index health.

## KPI definitions and proposed targets

All actual results are currently **not available**. Numeric targets should be set only after a verified site launch and a stable baseline (recommended first 28 complete days after instrumentation QA), commercial goals, inventory and seasonality are understood. Proposed targets are directional commitments to improve, not guaranteed results.

| KPI | Definition / measurement | Source | Frequency | Proposed target method | Business relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Organic clicks | Google Search clicks to TechFind pages; filter Pakistan where appropriate | GSC Performance | Weekly/monthly | Establish baseline; set quarterly growth goal after full period | Qualified search traffic |
| Organic impressions | Search appearances reported by Google | GSC Performance | Weekly/monthly | Track relevant query/page coverage | Visibility and demand capture |
| CTR | Clicks ÷ impressions for same filter/date scope | GSC | Monthly | Improve pages with meaningful impressions after checking query/position/SERP | Search-result appeal |
| Average position | GSC aggregate position metric; not exact rank | GSC | Monthly | Monitor trend by query/page, no fixed ranking promise | Directional visibility |
| Organic sessions/users | Sessions/users grouped by organic source/medium | GA4 | Weekly/monthly | Establish baseline and qualified-traffic objective | Site visits |
| Engaged sessions/rate | GA4 engagement definitions, consistently filtered | GA4 | Monthly | Use as diagnostic by landing page/device | Visit quality and usability |
| Ecommerce funnel | view_item → add_to_cart → begin_checkout → purchase counts/rates | GA4 + commerce backend reconciliation | Weekly/monthly | Set conversion targets only after event QA and margins/inventory confirmed | Purchase outcomes |
| Indexed intended pages | URLs intended for indexing and reported indexed/status distribution | GSC + URL inventory | Weekly/monthly | Resolve critical unintended exclusions; no blanket 100% target for all URLs | Discoverability |
| Core Web Vitals | Field status by eligible template/sample; keep lab separate | CrUX/PSI + Lighthouse | Monthly/on release | Set improvement goals from measured baseline | User experience |
| Referring domains/earned links | Unique domains and manually verified relevant editorial links | GSC Links/provider/manual log | Monthly | Quality and relevance goals after backlink baseline | Referral/discovery trust signals |
| Content performance | Impressions/clicks/sessions/events by editorial URL | GSC + GA4 | Monthly | Review opportunities and freshness by page intent | Content contribution |
| Qualified enquiries/purchases | Valid leads and completed orders; reconcile analytics with CRM/backend | GA4 + CRM/backend | Monthly | Use business-approved objective and attribution method | Revenue/customer value |

Do not combine GSC clicks with GA4 sessions as though they were the same event. Record reporting scope, date/time zone, country, device, search type, attribution settings, consent behavior and any measurement changes.

## Review cadence

- **Daily/automated:** availability, certificate/uptime and critical commerce tracking errors where monitoring exists.
- **Weekly:** GSC/GA4 anomalies, key query/page trends, indexing/technical alerts, release annotations.
- **Monthly:** full KPI dashboard, content and landing-page performance, link changes, technical health, actions and owners.
- **Quarterly:** strategy, business contribution, topic/page prioritization, conversion quality and next-quarter investments.
