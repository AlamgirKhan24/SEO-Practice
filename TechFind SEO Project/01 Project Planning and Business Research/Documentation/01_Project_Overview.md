# Module 01 — Project Overview

**Project:** TechFind | **Market:** Pakistan | **Status:** Planning-stage portfolio concept  
**Prepared:** 21 November 2026 | **Scope:** Module 01 only

## Executive summary

TechFind is a proposed online retailer and information resource for laptops and selected technology products in Pakistan. This module establishes a research-led foundation for later SEO work. There is currently no confirmed live domain, catalog, analytics history, or operating-business evidence in the project materials. Therefore, no actual customer, sales, ranking, traffic, conversion, or backlink claims are made.

The initial strategic direction is to make product choice safer and easier through clear specification data, genuine inventory and pricing, delivery/returns/warranty transparency, useful comparison guidance, and crawlable category and product pages. These are recommended differentiators to validate with the project owner and suppliers; they are not confirmed business capabilities.

## Business definition

- **Business model:** proposed B2C ecommerce retailer; optionally add B2B quote or bulk purchasing only if operationally supported.
- **Offer:** new laptops across supported brands and use cases, accessories, product advice, delivery, payment, and post-purchase support. Used/refurbished inventory is a possible extension only with clear condition grading and warranty rules.
- **Primary audience:** students, professionals/freelancers, gamers, and technology enthusiasts in Pakistan.
- **Commercial objectives:** build qualified demand, generate profitable online orders, grow product/category discovery, and earn trust/repeat purchase.
- **SEO objectives:** improve eligible URL indexation and organic visibility; attract relevant non-brand and brand visits; expose priority category/product inventory; and support measurable product views, add-to-cart, and purchases.

## Geographic and language scope

Start with Pakistan-wide targeting if nationwide fulfillment is real. Prioritize national category intent and service information. Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad/Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Peshawar, Multan, and Quetta are candidate demand/service areas, not a commitment to create a page for every city. City pages are warranted only when there is a distinct store, pickup/service footprint, or substantively different delivery offer. Use English as the initial content language because product model names and specifications are commonly expressed in English; assess Urdu query demand and customer preference before investing in Urdu pages. If translated pages are later introduced, translate fully, maintain language-specific URLs, and implement language/alternate relationships correctly. International sales are future scope only after fulfillment, duties, returns, payment, and support are established.

## Site and operating prerequisites

Confirm domain/canonical hostname, catalog and stock rules, genuine product sourcing, fulfillment coverage, payment options, return and warranty policy, platform/CMS, tracking and consent setup, support contacts, and approval/release workflow before implementation. A proposed site architecture is in `06_Website_Architecture.md`.

## Scope and control

This module covers business planning, audience hypotheses, desk research, geographic planning, objectives/KPIs, site architecture, initial strategy, tool planning, risks, and a full-project timeline. It does not begin competitor analysis, keyword research, or SEO implementation. Proceed to Module 02 only after explicit user instruction.
