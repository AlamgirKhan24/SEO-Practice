# Project Risks and Limitations

| Risk / current limitation | Impact | Practical response | Owner / checkpoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| No confirmed domain or live website in supplied materials | No crawl, indexation baseline, SERP URL review, or implementation verification | Confirm whether concept/mock/live; if live, provide domain and staging; if mock, label simulated work | Project owner, before Module 02/05 |
| No historical GSC/GA4 data | Cannot quantify baseline, seasonality, organic traffic, conversions, or results | Establish access and tracking before launch; capture dated baseline; report “not available” until then | Analytics owner, Module 09 |
| Catalog and supplier data unconfirmed | Category plan, product truth, stock and warranty promises may be inaccurate | Obtain product export and supplier evidence; define source of truth and update owner | Business owner, Module 01 follow-up |
| Paid SEO tools may be unavailable | Smaller competitor/backlink/keyword datasets and coarser estimates | Prioritize free Google/PTA/SBP data, public SERPs and limited crawler; record coverage and confidence | SEO lead, Modules 02–07 |
| Commercial laptop SERPs may be competitive | Ranking gains may require time, differentiation, authority, and strong operations | Research actual competition before prioritizing; focus on user value, technical quality and relevant inventory; no ranking promises | SEO lead, Module 02 onward |
| Import dependency and changing prices/variants | Stale or misleading pages, cancellations, support load | Model identifiers, verified feed cadence, stock/pricing QA, variant-level warranty disclosures | Merchandising, before launch |
| Service/warranty/payment promises not validated | Trust damage, customer disputes, regulatory exposure | Publish only approved operational facts; have business/legal review policies | Business/legal owner, before launch |
| Limited authoritative link opportunities | Slow authority growth; temptation toward low-quality link buying | Earn relevant coverage/assets and legitimate supplier citations; avoid paid schemes; assess actual baseline in Module 07 | SEO/content lead, Module 07 |
| CMS/developer constraints | URL, schema, performance, canonical and tracking changes can be delayed | Identify technical owner, staging, deployment windows, test/rollback procedure; prioritize high-impact template fixes | Tech lead, Module 05 |
| Faceted navigation and changing inventory | Duplicate/crawl-trap URLs or indexed empty categories | Review actual URL behavior; controlled crawl/index rules; maintain useful category/product states | Tech/merchandising, Module 05 |
| National service coverage is unknown | City-level promises or pages may mislead users | Confirm serviceable postcodes/couriers; avoid city pages absent unique local operation | Operations, Module 01 follow-up |
| Language demand unvalidated | Low-quality duplication or fragmented maintenance from premature Urdu rollout | Start with one well-maintained language; validate query/customer preference then plan full localization | Content owner, Modules 03/06 |
| Market sources are broad/dated for some topics | Laptop-specific market sizing and behavior remain uncertain | Mark dates and definitions; seek primary data and first-party research; do not extrapolate TAM from general ecommerce totals | Research owner, Modules 01/02 |

## Critical dependencies before claiming measurable outcomes

Live domain or clearly labeled simulated environment; verified data access; valid measurement implementation; agreed conversion definitions; a documented baseline period; stable stock and service policies; and a record of changes. Without these, report planned tasks and diagnostic findings only, not business impact.
