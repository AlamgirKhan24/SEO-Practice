## Trends and customer considerations

### Connectivity and device access

Broadband and mobile connectivity enable online product discovery, but a national subscriber total should not be read as individual addressable customers. Build a fast, mobile-friendly catalogue with compressed product imagery, legible specifications, and usable filters. Confirm performance on common mobile devices and variable network conditions in Module 05.

### Payments and trust

Payment channels are evolving, while the ecommerce guide also describes a cash-based/informal context. These sources are not directly comparable in scope: the SBP digital share covers retail transactions under its reporting definitions, while the ITA guide includes broad market commentary. Do not infer that all laptop buyers prefer prepaid or cash-on-delivery. TechFind should offer only supported payment methods, communicate security and reconciliation clearly, and measure checkout completion by payment option when consent and analytics rules permit.

### Import and catalog complexity

An import-dependent market increases the importance of variant accuracy, model identifiers, stock freshness, pricing/date changes, supplier validation, and clear warranty coverage. Product pages must not conflate similar regional configurations. Out-of-stock and discontinued pages need planned handling, not silent removal or misleading availability.

### Purchase risk and information needs

Laptop purchases involve trade-offs across performance, price, portability, battery, display, warranty, and intended workload. TechFind can reduce decision friction with complete specifications, real comparisons, usable filters, clear service policies, and helpful buying guides tied to actual inventory. These needs are strategic hypotheses; validate locally.

## How SEO can support growth

SEO can connect informational research to useful buying guidance, and high-intent category/product searches to accurate inventory. A crawlable architecture, strong internal links, descriptive titles/content, unique product data, and fast mobile experiences can improve eligibility and discoverability. Search visibility does not guarantee rankings or sales; stock, price, service quality, reputation, competition, and site quality also affect outcomes. Track impressions/clicks alongside qualified landing visits and ecommerce events.
