# TechFind — Module 03: Keyword Research & Strategy

**Research snapshot:** 21 November 2026  
**Target market:** Pakistan  
**Status:** Research candidates and proposed mapping; TechFind is still a proposed business without a confirmed domain/catalog.

## Executive summary

This module records **38 selected seed topics**, **150 unique keyword candidates**, and **110 long-tail candidates**. Google autocomplete phrases are preserved as observed wording, while the rest are explicitly marked as candidate expansions. One Google SERP sample supplied qualitative question ideas. No keyword volumes, organic difficulty, CPC, paid competition, rankings, or trend direction are claimed because no reliable metric export was available.

The first qualitative opportunities are laptop prices, stocked brand categories (HP, Dell, Lenovo), gaming and student use cases, and accessories. They are **conditional priorities**, not proven high-volume or easy-ranking terms. Before implementation, validate them against actual assortment, Pakistan SERPs, and a Keyword Planner/Ahrefs/Semrush export if available.

## Evidence and limitations

- Google autocomplete was observed on 21 Nov 2026 for laptop price, gaming laptop price, HP, Dell, students, and accessories queries. Google showed Pakistan context and a Malakand location inferred from IP. Suggestions can vary by time, location, and personalization. Their appearance does not equal demand or volume.
- The SERP sample for `best laptop price Pakistan` showed value/price questions (including value-for-money, cheapest, and brand-choice themes). It is one snapshot, not a ranking study.
- Google Trends Explore was opened for Pakistan, but chart data could not be read. No trend index or direction is reported.
- No Keyword Planner account/export, Ahrefs, Semrush, Search Console property, or TechFind domain was available. Volume, CPC, organic difficulty, paid auction competition, and first-party performance are unavailable.
- Competitor observations are limited to visible page/category wording from Module 02. No competitor keyword rankings or traffic are asserted.
- A number of plausible long-tail queries are research hypotheses, not verified autocomplete/searches. Use the source column in the database to distinguish these.

## Highest-priority clusters

| Priority | Cluster | Why it is prioritized | Launch gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| High, conditional | Laptop prices | Strong commercial intent and direct fit with laptop retail | Confirm the real catalog, currency/pricing, and a substantial laptop assortment |
| High, conditional | HP and Dell laptops | Clear brand plus price/browse intent; competitor taxonomies expose these routes | Create only for stocked, maintained brands with useful inventory |
| High, conditional | Gaming laptops | Valuable use-case browse intent | Confirm appropriate products and substantiate performance with specs |
| High, conditional | Student laptops | Audience/use-case fit and observed autocomplete support | Ensure products serve the chosen budget and student needs |
| High, conditional | Accessories and parts | Product and purchase intent | Verify assortment and compatibility data; separate parts/accessories only if supported |
| Medium | Buying guides and comparisons | Helpful consideration-stage content and internal-link support | Provide accurate, maintained advice and links to real products |
| Medium/low | Troubleshooting and service | Potentially useful support content | Publish only within expertise; don't imply a repair service that does not exist |

Priorities use relevance, intent, and business/page fit. They do **not** use numeric volume or difficulty because those metrics were unavailable. “High” is an editorial sequencing recommendation, not a forecast.

## Recommended page targeting

The full mapping is in `Analysis/09_Keyword_to_Page_Mapping.csv`. Proposed targets include:

- `/laptops/` → **laptop price in Pakistan**; supporting broad price/list queries.
- `/laptops/hp/` → **HP laptop price in Pakistan**.
- `/laptops/dell/` → **Dell laptop price in Pakistan**.
- `/laptops/lenovo/` → **Lenovo laptop price in Pakistan**.
- `/laptops/gaming/` → **gaming laptop price in Pakistan**.
- `/laptops/student/` → **laptop for students in Pakistan**.
- `/laptop-accessories/` → **laptop accessories in Pakistan**.
- `/guides/how-to-choose-a-laptop/` → **laptop buying guide Pakistan**.
- `/guides/hp-vs-dell-laptop/` → **HP vs Dell laptop Pakistan**.
- Exact product URLs → exact brand/model/configuration queries only after a sellable SKU and its specs are confirmed.

These URLs are illustrative patterns from Module 01, not live pages. Publish brand/use-case pages only when catalog depth supports a distinct, helpful landing page. Budget variants should generally be served as useful filters or supporting copy, not many thin indexable pages. Product pages should each have a single stable canonical URL.

## Implementation sequence (for approval and later work)

1. Confirm domain, live catalog, supported brands/use cases, price bands, accessories, and actual seller policies.
2. Obtain Pakistan-targeted Keyword Planner ranges or a paid-tool export if available; record date, geography, network, and match settings. Keep organic KD separate from Ads competition.
3. Recheck live Pakistan SERPs and query intent for the proposed primary pages; drop terms whose results or inventory do not fit.
4. Build the laptop hub and only the brand/use-case/category pages supported by real stock. Add accurate product details and verified trust/service information.
5. Publish one authoritative buying guide and comparison guide, internally linked to relevant categories. Add troubleshooting content only where TechFind can provide safe, competent advice.
6. Once a real domain exists, connect Search Console and use query/page data to refine targets. Monitor impressions, clicks, conversions, and cannibalization after launch.

This is a strategy handoff only. No on-page implementation or Module 04 work has been started.
