# Spreadsheet Export Notes

The requested XLSX workbooks could not be reliably exported with the workbook runtime in the previous project modules (the export failed with a fetch error). To preserve the research in usable form, this module provides UTF-8 CSV tables. Excel opens these files directly and parses their headers/rows. No XLSX artifacts are claimed as complete. CSVs are in `Research/` and `Analysis/` and cover the requested spreadsheet deliverables 1–9.
