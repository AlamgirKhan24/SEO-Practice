# TechFind Category Page SEO Template

**Status:** proposed reusable specification. Publish a category only when real catalog breadth and useful selection value exist.

## Page fields

- **Title:** `{Category} in Pakistan | Browse Available Models | TechFind` (adjust to the actual category; avoid claims such as “cheapest” without proof).
- **Meta description:** `Compare available {category} by verified specifications and current prices. Check stock and product details before you choose.`
- **H1:** `{Category} in Pakistan`.
- **Intro:** two to four concise sentences stating what the category includes, who it serves, and how to compare options. Mention only attributes truly represented in the listings.
- **Product grid:** consistent cards with product name, current price/currency, availability and concise verified spec highlights. Link product names/images with accessible labels.
- **Filters:** expose useful facets (brand, price, processor, memory, storage, display/use case) only when values are accurate. Keep filter state usable and accessible. URL crawl/index rules belong to Module 05; do not create indexable combinations by assumption.
- **Supporting content:** short selection advice or FAQs only when they answer real shopper questions and are accurate. Avoid boilerplate paragraphs copied across categories.
- **Internal links:** parent category, relevant stocked subcategories, buying guide and product details; use descriptive natural anchors.
- **Breadcrumb:** Home → Laptops → `{Category}` with functional links.

## Category-specific content safeguards

- HP/Dell/Lenovo: publish only for maintained inventory, describe available selection rather than making unsupported brand-wide claims.
- Gaming/student: explain objective criteria; don't label a product for a use case without appropriate specifications or evidence.
- Accessories: show compatibility constraints and included items; avoid implying a fit without verification.
- Budget: current prices change. Avoid fixed “under X” landing pages unless listings remain useful, in-stock, and distinctly curated; otherwise use a filter with carefully planned indexation.

## Example headings

- H1: `Gaming Laptops in Pakistan`
- H2: `Available gaming laptops`
- H2: `Compare graphics, processor and display`
- H2: `Choosing a gaming laptop`
- H3 under that section: `Check the games and software you use`; `Compare the full configuration`

Use one meaningful H1, then nested headings based on content structure. Do not choose headings only to insert keywords.

## QA before publishing

Check inventory depth, uniqueness from sibling categories, title/description uniqueness, price/stock accuracy, pagination/filter usability, image accessibility, mobile product grid, breadcrumbs, canonical/index directives and internal links. The latter technical directives should be verified during the technical SEO module, not inferred here.
