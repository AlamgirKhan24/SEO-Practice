# Daraz Pakistan SEO Case Study

**Website:** [Daraz.pk](https://www.daraz.pk/)  
**Evidence review date:** 21 November 2026  
**Project type:** Public-information learning case study

## Project status

This is an external SEO case study, not an implementation on Daraz. Public pages were reviewed for the initial business overview. No access to Daraz's Search Console, GA4, CMS, server logs, paid SEO-platform accounts, or internal business data has been provided. No private performance, ranking, traffic, backlink, conversion, crawl or keyword metrics are claimed.

The [project tracker](tracker/SEO_Project_Tracker.xlsx) contains 139 tasks across ten modules. Earlier module prompts and lessons exist in the chat, but practical exercises are not marked complete unless supporting evidence has been recorded here.

## Roadmap

| Module | Objective | Practical work | Core deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 — SEO Audit & Website Analysis | Understand the site and document SEO observations | Review public pages; inspect structure, crawl/index signals, metadata, headings, URLs, images, speed and mobile behavior | Business analysis, structure/audit reports, issue register, final audit |
| 02 — Keyword Research & Search Intent | Build a Pakistan-focused keyword strategy | Collect seed terms; classify intent; use Google suggestions and available research tools; group and map keywords | Keyword sheet, clusters, mapping, competitor gap and report |
| 03 — On-Page SEO | Evaluate page-level relevance and content elements | Review titles, descriptions, headings, page copy, images and links | On-page audit, examples and recommendations |
| 04 — Technical SEO | Assess crawling, indexation, rendering, performance and technical signals | Inspect robots.txt, sitemaps, canonicals, structured data, redirects, mobile and speed with authorized/public tools | Technical audit and prioritized issue list |
| 05 — Off-Page SEO | Assess authority and ethical external visibility opportunities | Review backlinks and mentions with available tool data; evaluate prospects and outreach ideas | Backlink evidence sheet and white-hat link plan |
| 06 — Content SEO & Content Strategy | Plan useful content that supports products and categories | Research topics, plan clusters, evaluate product/category copy and quality | Content inventory, calendar, briefs and strategy |
| 07 — Local SEO | Determine which local-search practices fit a national marketplace | Research location intent, eligibility, local pages and citations without assuming store eligibility | Local SEO assessment and recommendations |
| 08 — Competitor Analysis | Compare business and organic search competitors | Identify competitors from actual SERPs; compare structure, keywords, content, links and technical signals | Competitor matrix, gap analysis and strategy |
| 09 — SEO Performance Tracking | Define and report organic performance | Use authorized GSC/GA4 data if supplied; define KPIs, dashboard, cadence and alerts | Measurement framework, dashboard and report |
| 10 — Final Strategy & Documentation | Synthesize evidence into a portfolio-ready plan | Recheck findings, prioritize impact/effort, define owners/timeline, document limitations | Consolidated strategy, roadmap, GitHub documentation and portfolio case study |

## Working rules

1. Complete one task at a time and wait for learner confirmation before advancing.
2. Capture the URL, date, tool/method, observation, screenshot/export filename and limitations for each finding.
3. Label evidence as Verified, Potential, Inference, Recommendation or Unavailable.
4. Never fabricate scores, traffic, rankings, backlinks, crawl counts, volumes or technical errors.
5. Public site content changes. Save dated screenshots or exports for findings that may change.
6. Do not recommend implementation on Daraz: this is an external case study without site-owner authorization.
