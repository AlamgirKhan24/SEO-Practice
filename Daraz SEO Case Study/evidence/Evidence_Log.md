## Evidence classes

- **Verified:** directly observed in a dated page, tool output, export or screenshot.
- **Potential:** plausible issue/opportunity requiring another check.
- **Inference:** reasoned conclusion based on cited evidence; explain the reasoning.
- **Recommendation:** proposed future action, not proof of an existing issue.
- **Unavailable:** data or access was not supplied.

## Screenshot/export convention

Name files with date, page/tool and device/mode, for example: 2026-11-21_homepage_desktop_top.png or 2026-11-21_gsc_performance_export.csv. Record URL, date, device/viewport, tool and settings with the evidence. An unannotated screenshot is not a complete technical audit.
