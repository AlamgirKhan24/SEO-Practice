## Priority and severity guide

- **P1 — Urgent:** Confirmed issue that blocks important crawling/indexing or disrupts key pages. Address promptly and verify scope.
- **P2 — High:** Broad or high-impact issue affecting important templates or many valuable URLs.
- **P3 — Planned:** Material issue with limited scope, or an improvement that should be scheduled.
- **P4 — Backlog:** Low-impact cleanup or optional enhancement.

Severity describes the issue's potential effect; priority also considers affected pages, business importance, dependencies, and effort. Do not use a Lighthouse score or an SEO tool's health score as a substitute for impact analysis. Recommendations describe potential outcomes, not guaranteed ranking or traffic gains.
