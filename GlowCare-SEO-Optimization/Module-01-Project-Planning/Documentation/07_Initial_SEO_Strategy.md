# Initial SEO Strategy

This is a high-level plan only. No detailed keyword research, competitor analysis, crawl, or implementation is included in Module 01.

## How the work fits together
A verified catalog and customer needs inform site architecture; keyword research maps real intent to useful pages; technical foundations make those pages accessible and indexable; on-page content serves the intent accurately; helpful editorial content supports discovery and internal linking; ethical promotion can earn relevant awareness and links; measurement identifies what works and where to improve.

## Workstreams
- **Technical SEO:** Confirm platform, crawl/index controls, canonical and faceted URL rules, sitemap, redirects, mobile rendering, HTTPS, performance, structured data, and release QA.
- **On-page SEO:** Use unique descriptive titles, meta descriptions, heading hierarchy, concise category content, complete accurate product attributes, image handling, breadcrumbs, and contextual links.
- **Keyword research:** In Module 03, collect real suggestions and available metrics, classify intent, cluster terms, and map only to pages the catalog can support. No volume or difficulty is asserted here.
- **Content SEO:** Prioritize useful guides and buying resources, create evidence-reviewed briefs, provide authorship and sources, and review freshness. Avoid diagnosis, guaranteed outcomes, and unsupported product/ingredient claims.
- **Off-page SEO:** Build relationships with relevant publishers and organizations; seek editorially earned mentions; use supplier/brand listings only where genuine. Do not buy manipulative links or guarantee placements.
- **Local SEO:** Begin with national ecommerce intent. Do not create city pages solely to rank. Assess Google Business Profile eligibility only if GlowCare later operates a customer-facing location eligible under current Google rules.
- **Analytics:** Configure Search Console and consent-aware GA4; validate ecommerce events, internal traffic filters, currency, attribution, and data quality before reporting.

## Initial launch sequence
1. Confirm business model, product sourcing and facts, policies, language capacity, platform, and catalog.
2. Validate the customer and demand hypotheses.
3. Map architecture and query intent; define page templates.
4. Implement/QA technical, content, and analytics foundations.
5. Publish priority pages with accurate product data and useful supporting content.
6. Monitor indexing, CWV, search queries, landing-page engagement, product actions, and conversions; iterate from evidence.
