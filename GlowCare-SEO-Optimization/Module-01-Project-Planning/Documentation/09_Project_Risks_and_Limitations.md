# Project Risks and Limitations

| Risk | Why it matters | Practical response |
|---|---|---|
| Strong competition | Established retailers and marketplaces may have brand, inventory, and authority advantages | Differentiate through useful information and service that can be delivered; prioritize feasible query/page opportunities after research |
| Product authenticity concerns | Trust-sensitive category; unsubstantiated claims create reputational and legal exposure | Verify supply chain, invoices, batch/expiry records, and brand permissions; publish only evidence-backed sourcing statements |
| Missing product information | Incomplete ingredients, size, directions, warnings, or origin undermines usefulness and accuracy | Require a product-data checklist and source documents before publication |
| Unsupported skincare claims | Health or efficacy assertions may mislead and require substantiation/review | Use neutral factual descriptions; evidence and qualified review for claims; avoid diagnosis and guaranteed outcomes |
| Limited SEO data | No site, Search Console, GA4, query exports, or customer research exists | Treat targets as provisional; establish baselines only after verified setup; label estimates and simulations |
| Limited budget | Paid tooling, content review, development, and logistics compete for resources | Start with free tools and a narrow validated assortment; prioritize high-impact templates and essential analytics |
| Technical platform limits | URL control, structured data, speed, rendering, or analytics may be restricted | Evaluate platform before commitment; test a representative template; document developer dependencies |
| Lack of customer reviews | New store may have limited social proof | Request genuine post-purchase feedback ethically; never fabricate reviews or markup; use other substantiated trust evidence |
| Limited backlinks | New domain may lack authority signals | Earn mentions through useful resources and legitimate outreach; avoid paid link schemes |
| No live business data | Inventory, prices, suppliers, geography, and sales remain unspecified | Keep all business content conceptual until confirmed; do not publish proposed facts as real claims |
| Shipping/payment uncertainty | Coverage, failed delivery cost, COD and returns affect customer experience and margins | Confirm courier SLAs, current payment mix, serviceable areas, delivery charges, and returns before promising terms |
| Regulatory uncertainty | Cosmetics rules and advertising requirements can change and depend on product/model | Obtain current Pakistan-specific legal/regulatory review before sale and before publishing regulated claims |
| Language quality | Poor translation can confuse shoppers and weaken trust | Publish Urdu/English only where reviewed by fluent editors and support can serve the language |

## Limitations
This is desk research and planning, not a live SEO audit, legal opinion, market sizing study, customer survey, or implemented business plan. Some sources are secondary, geographically limited, dated, or focused on premium cosmetics. See the market research file for source-specific caveats.
