# Target Audience Research — planning personas

These are provisional archetypes built from the proposed offer and limited Pakistan-specific published research. They are not interview findings, verified segment sizes, or claims about all consumers. Research includes a Lahore young-adult social-commerce survey emphasizing information quality, influencer credibility, and trust, and a Karachi premium beauty study that notes authenticity concerns. A 2018–19 Abbottabad study found most respondents used offline channels; its age, location, sample, and date limit generalization. [Lahore study](https://ijournalar.com/site/index.php/ijar/article/view/1518) · [IBA Karachi premium beauty study](https://ir.iba.edu.pk/sbselp/81/) · [Abbottabad study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10986314/)

| Persona | Needs and behavior hypothesis | Search/content needs | Product interests |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ayesha, student / early-career researcher | Price-aware; may compare options and seek clear basic product facts; age range 18–24 is an illustrative planning segment, not a proven dominant segment | Simple explainers, comparison tables, ingredient definitions, usage instructions; searches may mix English and Urdu/Roman Urdu | Cleanser, moisturizer, sunscreen, lip care; only when supported by catalog |
| Sana, busy professional | Values convenient replenishment, delivery clarity, mobile usability, and quick product comparisons | Concise category filters, delivery/returns details, routine explainers | Familiar daily-use products and travel formats if available |
| Mariam, ingredient-conscious enthusiast | Reads labels and compares formulation details; may be skeptical of broad claims | Detailed product attributes, sourced educational pages, transparent limitations, editorial reviews | Serums, toners, masks, targeted categories; no treatment promise |
| Hira, first-time online beauty shopper | May need seller/product confidence, payment choices, and clear support; this is a hypothesis to test | Authenticity/sourcing evidence, payment and delivery FAQs, customer service information | Entry products and familiar brands, depending on verified sourcing |
| Farah, household/personal-care shopper | May buy for multiple household needs and value availability, clear policies, and predictable delivery | Category navigation, straightforward product labels, Urdu/English help where feasible | Body care, hair care, sunscreen, family-appropriate basics; suitability claims must be substantiated |

### Research questions to validate
- Which languages/scripts customers use for product discovery (English, Urdu script, Roman Urdu) and how this varies by query.
- Which trust signals shoppers need and which GlowCare can substantiate.
- Budget ranges, product replenishment cycles, preferred payment, delivery expectations, and acceptable return rules.
- Whether customers prefer brand-led, ingredient-led, concern-led, or routine-led navigation.
- Whether the proposed categories reflect real demand and supply.

Do not treat age, gender, occupation, or skin type as a targeting proxy without evidence. Avoid inferring skin concerns or medical needs from persona labels.
