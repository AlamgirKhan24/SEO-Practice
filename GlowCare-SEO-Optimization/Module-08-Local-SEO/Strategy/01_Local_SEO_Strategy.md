# Local SEO Strategy — GlowCare Pakistan

**Status:** Proposed local discovery plan. GlowCare is currently a proposed online store; no physical storefront, in-person customer service, delivery coverage or city-level demand data has been confirmed.

## Strategic fit
Prioritize national organic ecommerce SEO and clear delivery information. Local SEO becomes appropriate only where real operations support it: a staffed customer-facing location or an eligible service-area operation that makes in-person contact. Delivery nationwide by courier alone does not establish Google Business Profile eligibility.

Google’s current Business Profile rules require in-person customer contact during stated hours; online-only businesses are listed as ineligible. [Google eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036) · [Get started](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7039811)

## Geographic approach
Use Pakistan-wide targeting for category, product and guides initially. Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad/Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Faisalabad and Multan are reasonable operational markets to investigate, but 2023 census population context does not prove beauty search demand or sales potential. Only build city pages if delivery/service facts are distinct, accurate and useful. Otherwise use one national shipping page with verified service coverage.

## Local customer intent
Queries with city modifiers may express intent to find nearby shops, same-city delivery, local stock or pickup. GlowCare must not imply a local store or same-day delivery unless real operations substantiate it. Build local content around shipping availability, delivery times, fees, pickup (if any), returns and customer support.

## Local trust and content
Use accurate legal/business identity, verified contact channels, transparent fulfilment terms, and genuine reviews. Local seasonal advice must rely on reliable sources and avoid unsupported dermatological claims. No profile/listing, city page, citation or review is currently live.

## Measurement
Use GSC query/page/country data and GA4 landing pages after setup; city performance may be approximate due to privacy/consent and geolocation. Business Profile metrics apply only if eligible and verified. No local volumes or outcomes are claimed.
