# Conditional Google Business Profile Setup Guide

**Do not apply while GlowCare remains online-only.** Use this checklist only after a real eligible business operation is documented and current Google policies are reviewed.

1. Confirm in-person customer contact during stated business hours and a permitted business type.
2. Use the real-world business name consistently; no keyword stuffing.
3. Select the closest accurate primary category; add secondary categories only for real services.
4. Show a public address only for an eligible staffed location customers can visit. For a qualifying service-area business, follow current address hiding/service-area guidance; do not invent or expose a residential address improperly.
5. Add verified local phone, website and hours. Do not use call centers or numbers without control.
6. Describe actual products/services without unsupported claims.
7. Add genuine photos of the real location/team/products only with rights and truthful context.
8. Complete Google’s verification process; do not create duplicates.
9. Request honest reviews from all eligible customers neutrally, without incentives or gating; respond professionally.
10. Keep profile details synchronized with the website and citations; audit changes periodically.

Fields such as address, phone, category, hours, service area, photos and verification status remain unconfirmed for GlowCare. See [Google eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036) and [Business Profile policies](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177).
