# Local Reviews and Reputation Management

GlowCare has no confirmed customers or reviews. Do not seed testimonials or claim an existing reputation.

## Ethical review process (if/when operating)
- Invite all customers neutrally to share an honest review after a completed purchase using a consistent post-purchase message.
- Do not offer discounts, gifts or compensation in exchange for reviews; do not selectively solicit only satisfied customers or suppress negative feedback.
- Never write, buy or fabricate reviews. Do not ask staff/family to pose as customers.
- Make it easy to contact support separately for order issues while preserving a genuine public-review option.
- Respond calmly, avoid revealing order/customer personal data, acknowledge the concern, and offer an appropriate private resolution route.
- Monitor platform rules and applicable Pakistan consumer/ad disclosure requirements.

## Response templates
**Positive:** “Thank you for sharing your experience. We appreciate the feedback and will pass it to the team.”

**Negative:** “Thank you for letting us know. We’re sorry this did not meet expectations. Please contact [verified support channel] with your order reference so we can review it. Please don’t post personal or payment information publicly.”

Track review count/rating only on platforms that allow measurement, response time, recurring themes, and resolved issues. Do not treat review markup as a way to obtain search stars.
