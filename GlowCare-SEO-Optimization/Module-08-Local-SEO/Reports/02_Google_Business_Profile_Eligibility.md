# Google Business Profile Eligibility Assessment

## Current determination: Not established; do not create a profile now
GlowCare is described in Modules 01–07 as a proposed ecommerce store. No physical shop customers can visit, staffed location, in-person service, customer visit process, or service-area operation has been confirmed.

Google’s eligibility guidance says a business must make in-person contact with customers during stated hours; it specifically lists online-only businesses as ineligible. A courier-delivery ecommerce business does not become eligible merely by delivering products to cities. [Eligibility rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036) · [Business Profile help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7039811)

## Reassess only if operations change
Potential eligibility requires evidence of real in-person customer contact, such as a staffed customer-facing retail location during stated hours or a qualifying service-area business that visits customers. A warehouse, virtual office, mailbox or unstaffed pickup point should not be represented as a storefront without confirmation against current Google policy.

## If ineligible
Focus on organic ecommerce SEO, accurate national service/dispatch information, website contact details, social channels and legitimate citations that match the real business. Do not create a fake profile, address or city landing pages claiming branches.

Eligibility is a policy assessment based on currently described facts, not legal advice. Recheck Google’s current policies before any listing creation.
