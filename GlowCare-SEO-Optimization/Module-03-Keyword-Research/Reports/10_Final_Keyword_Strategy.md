# Final Keyword Strategy — GlowCare Pakistan

**Research date:** 21 November 2026  
**Project status:** Proposed portfolio store; no live GlowCare domain, catalog, Search Console, or Ads account access was provided.

## Evidence and limitations
This phase creates a structured candidate set using the Module 01 architecture and the public competitor category/page patterns reviewed in Module 02. Search patterns include commercial forms such as product + Pakistan/online/price and informational forms such as routine, label and ingredient questions. These are keyword hypotheses, not measured demand.

Google Trends’ Pakistan interface is available for relative interest exploration, and Keyword Planner can provide account-dependent forecasts/ranges. Neither tool was accessed for exact terms or exported metrics in this work. Google Search autocomplete, Related Searches, and People Also Ask were not captured as a reproducible query-by-query dataset. Ahrefs and Semrush were not available. Therefore no volume, CPC, paid competition, organic difficulty, or ranking positions are claimed. Metrics workbook marks them unavailable. The keyword set must not be represented as search-volume validated.

Sources/tools for next validation: [Google Trends — Pakistan](https://trends.google.com/trending?geo=pk&hl=en), [Google Ads Keyword Planner](https://ads.google.com/home/tools/keyword-planner/), and the official competitor/category pages listed in Module 02. Data collection date should be recorded per export.

## Candidate set
The workbooks contain 61 seed keywords, 240 expanded candidate phrases and 50 long-tail candidates. Expanded phrases use controlled product, geography and purchase modifiers; all need validation against real suggestions, business inventory, language usage and available metrics. The list is not a claim that all phrases have search demand.

## Target first (conditional)
1. Core transactional categories: skincare products, cleansers, moisturizers, sunscreens and serums, but only where the store has real, useful inventory.
2. Product pages for actual stocked products with complete verified data, rather than generic placeholder pages.
3. Commercial investigation guides such as sunscreen buying/label guide and moisturizer comparison, after claims and reviewer process are approved.
4. Beginner and ingredient explainers that answer real questions and cite authoritative sources.
5. Brand pages only for genuine authorization/stock and distinct user value.

The provisional prioritization score combines business relevance and intent. Search demand and difficulty are intentionally excluded until valid data are collected; consequently “High Priority” is a planning rank, not a ranking prediction.

## Page and cannibalization rules
- Assign one primary intent/page to each topic cluster. A category serves broad transactional product discovery; an editorial guide serves learning/comparison intent; a product page serves the exact product.
- Do not create separate near-duplicate pages for word-order or “online Pakistan” variations. Use natural language on the best-fitting page.
- Keep brand navigation distinct from general category content. Do not create brand pages for brands GlowCare does not sell.
- Use canonical and faceted URL rules once platform behavior is known.
- Review Urdu-script and Roman Urdu query patterns before building localized pages; publish translations only with fluent editorial and support capacity.

## Internal links
Use breadcrumbs from product to category and home; link between related categories where relevant; link buying guides to suitable categories and only available products; link product pages back to brand/category and relevant factual guides. Avoid repetitive exact-match anchor text.

## Phased implementation roadmap
**Phase A — Validate (before site build):** Confirm catalog, suppliers, country-wide availability, product claims and policies. Review seed set and add customer/support language.

**Phase B — Measure demand (Module 03 refinement):** Use Keyword Planner with Pakistan targeting, Google Trends comparisons and dated autocomplete/PAA capture. If accessible, cross-check Ahrefs/Semrush estimates. Label Ads competition separately from organic difficulty. Maintain raw exports in Research.

**Phase C — Build architecture:** Select only supported categories; map each cluster to one page; define indexation and filter treatment with the developer.

**Phase D — Launch priorities:** Publish core categories and complete product pages; add measurement; monitor indexing and query/page data.

**Phase E — Expand content:** Use validated informational demand and support questions to publish evidence-reviewed guides. Assess performance quarterly and refresh priorities based on GSC impressions/clicks, organic landing engagement, product views, add-to-cart and purchases.
