## Project overview

This ten-module project demonstrates an ecommerce SEO planning workflow: business assumptions, competitor sampling, keyword hypotheses, page recommendations, technical planning, content development, ethical link prospecting, local eligibility, and analytics design. Objectives are to plan a measurable future SEO foundation, map validated intent to useful pages, recommend accurate content, and define verification methods.

Audience needs (product/routine discovery, ingredient education, authenticity, and Pakistan-specific delivery information) are hypotheses requiring customer, catalogue, and operational validation. Broad digital/ecommerce context does not establish GlowCare-specific demand.

Methods and artifacts include cited market context, public competitor page samples, keyword ideation/clustering, page mapping, technical checklists, content planning, spreadsheet workbooks, and blank analytics templates. GSC, GA4, a crawler, PageSpeed Insights/Lighthouse, schema validators, and keyword/backlink platforms are future validation options only; active access/use is not claimed.

### Module 09 dashboard review

The workbook has **Dashboard** and **Monthly Inputs** sheets. Dashboard fields cover current/previous period, source/date/notes and deltas. Monthly inputs cover GSC clicks/impressions/CTR/position, GA4 organic sessions/users/engaged sessions, add-to-cart, checkout starts, purchases, revenue, export date and notes. The range is an autofiltered table; a line chart plots monthly clicks. Inputs are blank. Visibility, traffic and conversion fields exist; charting is limited to clicks. Keyword, content, technical, backlink and local KPIs are in separate trackers rather than integrated. Before use, confirm formulas in Excel, do not treat blanks as zeros, preserve period/source/date/currency, and distinguish GSC average position from rank-tracker data. No data or edits were made.

## Portfolio case study

A ten-module planning project designed an organic search foundation for a proposed skincare ecommerce concept. The central challenge was the absence of a live site, catalogue, business operations, analytics, and client data. Assumptions are labeled and recommendations are separated from verified implementation.

I documented market context and audience hypotheses, sampled competitor pages, built keyword candidates and intent/page mapping, prepared on-page templates and technical audit plans, developed content pillars/briefs/drafts, planned ethical link prospecting, reviewed local eligibility, and created a GSC/GA4 framework with a blank dashboard.

**Deliverables:** nine module folders of research, workbooks, templates, draft content, strategy reports and tracking plans, plus final QA, evidence reporting, portfolio copy, README and roadmap.  
**Skills:** ecommerce SEO planning; competitor/keyword research; intent clustering/page mapping; technical/on-page SEO; content planning; ethical link prospecting; local eligibility; analytics design; evidence-led reporting.  
**Tools:** Markdown and spreadsheets. GSC, GA4, crawling, performance and research tools are future verification methods, not claimed as used.  
**Limitations:** no verified business/client relationship, live changes, published content, rankings, traffic, backlinks, conversions, revenue, or growth.  
**Conclusion:** The project demonstrates a connected planning workflow and transparent evidence handling. Future work needs a real business, domain, catalogue, implementation access, and measurable baseline.
