# Content SEO Strategy — GlowCare Pakistan

**Status:** Proposed content program for a portfolio concept. No pages are live or published. Keyword metrics in Module 03 remain unavailable, so editorial priorities are intent and business-fit hypotheses, not demand forecasts.

## Objectives and audience
Support product discovery and organic visibility with useful category, product and educational content; help shoppers compare product facts and understand labels; build transparent trust and guide readers to relevant stocked products. Planning personas from Module 01 include student/early-career researchers, busy professionals, ingredient-conscious enthusiasts, first-time online beauty shoppers, and household shoppers. These are archetypes to validate, not demographic facts.

## Intent and content formats
- Transactional: category/product pages with accurate catalog data, filters, stock and clear delivery/returns.
- Commercial investigation: buying guides and comparisons with transparent criteria, current data, limitations and no universal “best” claims.
- Informational: practical guides, label/ingredient explainers and routines grounded in reliable sources.
- Navigational: real brand pages, About/Contact and policies; no claims of authorization without proof.

## Priorities
1. Verify catalog, sourcing, product documentation, policies, and reviewer capacity.
2. Create/optimize priority category and product page content from verified inputs.
3. Publish a small group of useful foundational guides, then expand only after query/customer evidence.
4. Link guides to suitable categories/products naturally, and make policies/support easy to find.

## Content pillars
Skincare basics; cleansers; moisturizers; sunscreens; serums/ingredients; toners/masks; routines; product selection; hair care; body care. Topic cluster detail is in `04_Topic_Clusters_and_Content_Pillars.md`.

## Content operations
Each brief defines intent, audience, outline, links, sources and CTA. A qualified editor/reviewer checks factual and health-adjacent content before publication. Use GSC/GA4 only after verified implementation; report clicks, impressions, CTR, landing sessions and commerce outcomes without inventing results. Maintain content sources, owner, review date and changes.

## Distribution and maintenance
Repurpose approved guides into platform-native educational snippets, use email only with consent, collaborate transparently, and participate in communities only when relevant and permitted. Review product facts as catalog changes; review educational sources quarterly and refresh based on factual change or Search Console evidence.

## Limits
No live site, inventory, author credentials, market interviews, GSC/GA4 access or validated query metrics exist. The 90-day calendar dates are a proposed schedule, not commitments or publication records. Content gap evidence from Module 02 is a limited sample, not exhaustive competitor coverage.
