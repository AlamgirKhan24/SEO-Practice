# Module 06 — Final Content SEO Report

## Overview
This module develops a proposed content program for GlowCare, a portfolio concept targeting Pakistan. It uses Module 01’s audience/architecture hypotheses; Module 02’s limited competitor page sample; Module 03’s candidate keyword map with unavailable demand metrics; Module 04’s content templates and trust rules; and Module 05’s no-live-site limitation. No material is claimed published.

## Deliverables and findings
- Content inventory workbook covers core page types and marks status proposed.
- Search intent mapping connects candidate terms to pages/formats while preserving one primary intent per URL.
- Topic cluster map covers ten content pillars.
- Gap workbook lists 24 evidence-qualified opportunities; competitor absence is not asserted without a full crawl.
- 90-day calendar proposes one item per week; dates are planning placeholders.
- Ten briefs and three original article drafts are ready for editor and subject review.
- Editorial, on-page checklist, distribution, KPI and maintenance systems are included.

## Measurement
Use GSC for impressions, clicks, CTR and average position by query/page; use GA4 for organic landing sessions, engagement and assisted/ecommerce outcomes after consent-aware configuration and event QA. Monitor indexing in GSC. No performance baseline or results exist.

## Limitations
No live site, catalog, product documentation, author/reviewer credentials, query metrics, customer interviews, analytics access or publication schedule was supplied. External source search was unavailable during drafting; cited authoritative references should be checked for currency, exact claim fit and Pakistan applicability by an editor before release. Calendar dates and keyword targets are proposed only.
