## Cluster map (conceptual)
Home → category pillar → product pages. Guides hub → educational pillar → supporting guides. Each guide links to one relevant category and selectively to products; product pages link back to category, brand (if stocked), shipping/returns, and relevant factual guide. One page owns each primary intent to reduce cannibalization.
