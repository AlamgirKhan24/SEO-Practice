# Content Gap Analysis — Evidence and Opportunities

Module 02 sampled five retailer/brand sites rather than crawling every competitor. It observed category/skin-type navigation and a blog link at Skincare Pakistan; product concern merchandising, ratings and a matching tool at Jenpharm; guides/blog and skincare navigation at Yellow Pink; and deep category/attribute filters at Naheed. These are specific observations, not proof that competitors lack any topic. Candidate gaps below are opportunities to validate, not established market whitespace.

See workbook `05_Content_Gap_Analysis.xlsx` for 24 opportunities with candidate terms, intent, evidence caveat, format and priority. Highest-fit opportunities include label-reading, sunscreen label education, beginner routines, product comparisons with transparent criteria, online buying checks, and product data/expiry transparency. Search volume is unavailable; validate with customer questions and query tools.
