# Content Distribution Strategy

Content is planned, not published. Distribution should begin only after editorial review and live URL approval.

| Channel | Role | Practical use | Guardrails / measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Visual discovery and short education | Carousel summaries, label-reading tips, short clips linking to full guide | No medical advice in comments; disclose sponsorship; measure referral sessions/saves |
| Facebook | Broad community and customer updates | Share useful guide excerpts and policy/product updates | Follow group rules; no repetitive promotion; track referral clicks |
| Pinterest | Evergreen visual discovery | Pin checklists/diagrams to relevant boards | Use accurate titles and destination URLs; avoid implying treatment outcomes |
| LinkedIn | Professional brand and portfolio credibility | Share research process, content standards and ecommerce learning | Keep professional claims factual; no inflated results |
| Email newsletter | Retention and useful updates | Send approved guides, product education and catalog updates to opted-in subscribers | Consent, unsubscribe and privacy requirements; monitor clicks/unsubscribes |
| Online communities | Answer genuine questions | Participate as a transparent brand representative where permitted; answer helpfully and link only when relevant | No astroturfing, fake accounts, undisclosed promotion or scraped contacts |
| Influencer collaboration | Reach and demonstration | Work with suitable creators for clear, accurate product education | Written disclosure, truthful claims, usage rights, no scripted fake testimonials |

Repurpose one reviewed guide into channel-native assets; do not copy-paste the entire article everywhere. Use campaign-tagged links consistently and separate paid/owned/earned performance.
