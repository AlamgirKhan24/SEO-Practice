# Skincare Editorial Guidelines

## Source standards
Use current primary or authoritative sources: recognized dermatology organizations, government health/regulatory agencies, peer-reviewed research and product manufacturers for exact product facts. Distinguish regulatory labeling definitions by country. Keep source URL, publication/update date, access date and exact claim supported in an editorial log. Do not cite search snippets or competitor claims as medical evidence.

## Product and ingredient facts
Use exact manufacturer/package data for INCI, size, directions, warnings and origin. Record version/date and verify before publishing. Do not infer an ingredient’s effect, concentration or safety from its name alone. Avoid converting cosmetic marketing claims into treatment claims.

## Safety and health boundaries
Explain general information, not diagnosis or personalized medical care. Do not promise cures, guaranteed results or timelines. For persistent symptoms, reactions or medical conditions, advise readers to consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional. Do not recommend stopping prescribed treatment.

## Sunscreen
Use jurisdiction-appropriate government/dermatology sources; explain only the terms on the product’s actual label. Avoid “waterproof,” universal rankings, or time-in-sun calculations. Distinguish lab label information from individual real-world use.

## Authors and review
Display actual author names and relevant experience only with verification and consent. Assign a qualified reviewer for health-adjacent content when needed; record reviewer, scope and date. Do not invent dermatologist endorsement or credentials.

## Updates and citations
Recheck sources and claims at least quarterly, and sooner when authoritative guidance, regulations, products or policies change. Cite near the claim; link to the original source. Keep a change log and visible reviewed date when meaningful.

## Tone and accessibility
Use plain, inclusive language; define technical terms; avoid shame, fear, and skin-color bias. Add alt text that describes meaningful images. Offer reviewed Urdu translations only when translation and support capacity exist.
