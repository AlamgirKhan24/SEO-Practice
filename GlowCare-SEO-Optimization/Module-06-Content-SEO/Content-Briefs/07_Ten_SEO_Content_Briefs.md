- Intent/audience/funnel: Informational; new skincare shoppers; awareness.

- Meta: Learn how to organize a beginner routine with clear product steps, label guidance and reliable sources.

- Outline: H2 Start with product labels; H2 Keep steps simple; H2 Follow manufacturer directions (H3 introduce one product at a time only if source supports); H2 When to ask a clinician; H2 FAQs and sources.

- Images: simple routine-step diagram, alt text. CTA: browse available categories. Quality: no diagnosis or results claims.
