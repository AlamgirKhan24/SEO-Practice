# GlowCare SEO Optimization

A ten-module SEO portfolio demonstration for **GlowCare**, a proposed skincare and beauty ecommerce concept targeting Pakistan.

> **Disclosure:** This repository documents research, strategy, templates and draft content. GlowCare is not established here as a real client or operating business. No live implementation or SEO performance results are claimed.

## Purpose and objectives

Demonstrate an evidence-conscious ecommerce SEO workflow: research business context, map candidate search intent to page types, recommend on-page/technical/content work, plan ethical authority-building, assess local eligibility, and design measurement.

## Modules

1. Project planning and business research
2. Competitor analysis
3. Keyword research and strategy
4. On-page SEO
5. Technical SEO
6. Content SEO
7. Off-page SEO
8. Local SEO
9. SEO monitoring and analytics
10. Final documentation

## Deliverables and methods

Market/context research; qualitative competitor samples; keyword candidate inventories and mapping; on-page templates; technical audit plans and placeholder examples; content pillars, briefs, calendar and unpublished drafts; ethical outreach frameworks; conditional local SEO; GSC/GA4 guides and blank dashboard; final QA, evidence report and roadmap.

The work uses Markdown and spreadsheet files, cited contextual research, public page sampling, keyword ideation/clustering, and planning templates. GSC, GA4, crawling, PageSpeed Insights/Lighthouse and research platforms are future validation methods, not confirmed active properties or tools used.

## Data accuracy policy

- No invented search volumes, rankings, traffic, backlinks, conversions, revenue or growth.
- Candidate keywords and priorities remain hypotheses until supported by dated data.
- A recommendation is not represented as implemented without evidence.
- Blank analytics fields are not zeros.
- Product/health claims need accurate evidence and review.
- Record source, date, geography and method for refreshed research.

## Limitations and future work

No verified domain/store, catalogue, client relationship, site access, GSC/GA4 properties, crawl, rank export, backlink dataset, eligible local address, or sales data was supplied. Technical examples contain placeholders; content is unpublished.

Future work depends on confirming the business and platform, validating demand and product facts, capturing live baselines, implementing and QAing recommendations, publishing reviewed content, configuring privacy-aware analytics, and reporting from dated evidence.
