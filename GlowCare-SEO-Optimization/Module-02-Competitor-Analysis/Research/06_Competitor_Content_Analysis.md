# Competitor Content Analysis

**Review date:** 21 November 2026. Sampled publicly accessible homepages, category pages, and any content areas exposed in search or navigation. This is a directional sample, not a full content inventory.

## Observed patterns
- Skincare Pakistan uses shop-by-category and shop-by-skin-type navigation and a blog link, alongside brand and bestseller discovery. [Site](https://skincarepakistan.pk/)
- Jenpharm organizes homepage merchandising around product types/concerns, visible ratings, customer reviews, bundles and a “Find Your Perfect Match” feature. Site language includes efficacy claims; GlowCare should not imitate claim strength without appropriate substantiation. [Site](https://jenpharm.com/)
- Yellow Pink combines skincare and Korean skincare merchandising with health/wellness categories, customer-review snippets, COD, authenticity and return messages. [Site](https://www.yellowpink.pk/)
- Naheed’s category structure supports broad attribute/brand/category filtering, demonstrating utility for large catalogs. [Category](https://www.naheed.pk/health-beauty/skin-care)
- CoNatural presents own-brand product categories and bundles, suitable for brand-led cross-sell rather than neutral multi-brand education. [Site](https://conaturalintl.com/)

## Content gaps/opportunities for GlowCare
Build content around verified product facts and shopper decisions rather than unsupported treatment promises. The following 18 opportunities are topic hypotheses only; validate demand and query wording in Module 03, then source-check and review before publication:

1. How to read a skincare product label: size, ingredients, directions and warnings.
2. Cleanser formats explained: gel, cream and micellar water (neutral, product-fact based).
3. Moisturizer textures and how to compare them.
4. Sunscreen label terms explained from authoritative sources; avoid overpromising protection.
5. How to layer products according to manufacturer directions; note when advice is general.
6. Beginner routine planning with minimal steps and no medical advice.
7. How to patch test a cosmetic product, with authoritative references.
8. Product storage and expiry/period-after-opening labels explained.
9. How to compare serum ingredient lists without ranking efficacy.
10. What “fragrance-free” and “unscented” mean, with source citations.
11. Skincare travel packing checklist, subject to airline rules.
12. How to choose a cleanser by format and personal preference.
13. What to check before buying imported cosmetics online: seller, label, batch and return terms.
14. How to compare product size and unit price.
15. Routine planner by product step, not by claimed diagnosis.
16. Hair/body care product categories guide tied to actual stock.
17. Sunscreen reapplication instructions summarized from reliable public-health sources.
18. A glossary of common cosmetic label terms with clear limits on what the terms establish.

Each page needs a clear audience, qualified reviewer where needed, primary/authoritative sources, a non-medical scope, and links to relevant available products. Do not state a “content gap” as proven without content inventory and query evidence.
