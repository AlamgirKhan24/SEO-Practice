# GlowCare SWOT — Competitor Review

## Strengths (proposed, not verified)
- Proposed single destination can curate skincare and adjacent care categories.
- Can design clear category architecture and product data from the outset.
- Can prioritize transparent product facts, sourcing evidence, and useful education as planned differentiators.

## Weaknesses (current project state)
- No live website, catalog, suppliers, operating history, customer reviews, or analytics.
- No verified sourcing or fulfillment advantage yet.
- New brand would lack established search visibility, links, and customer confidence.

## Opportunities (evidence-informed)
- Competitors demonstrate category and skin-type navigation, broad filters, promotional merchandising, reviews, support and delivery messaging; GlowCare can implement a focused, well-structured version appropriate to its actual range.
- Local studies cited in Module 01 and retailer messaging make authenticity/sourcing information a relevant trust subject, but neither establishes national concern prevalence.
- Useful label, product comparison and shopping-safety education can serve research journeys if independently sourced and carefully reviewed.

## Threats
- Established retailers and own-brand businesses compete for product, brand and category intent.
- Marketplace listings create price and assortment pressure, with seller quality varying by listing.
- Authenticity allegations, counterfeit risk, supply instability, expiry and customer-service failures could damage trust.
- Regulatory and advertising rules, platform changes and search competition can affect content and operations.

This is a strategic SWOT for a concept, not an audited company assessment. Market and site observations are documented in the supporting analysis and workbooks.
