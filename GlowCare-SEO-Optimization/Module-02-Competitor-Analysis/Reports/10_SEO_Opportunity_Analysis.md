# SEO Opportunity Analysis — GlowCare

**Evidence date:** 21 November 2026. This prioritization is based on sampled public competitor pages, not keyword-volume data, a live GlowCare site, or a full competitor crawl. Priority reflects practical value and dependency, not guaranteed ranking impact.

| Priority | Opportunity / evidence | SEO value and business relevance | Recommended action | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | Verify actual sourcing, catalog, product facts, delivery and return terms before claims; competitors surface these prominently | Trust and accurate product data are foundational in a skincare purchase journey | Create approved product-data/sourcing record and publish only verifiable claims | High (operations/legal dependencies) |
| Critical | No GlowCare domain/catalog/analytics provided | Cannot map pages, measure results, or validate competitor gaps | Confirm business model, domain, platform, catalog and GSC/GA4 access before execution | Medium |
