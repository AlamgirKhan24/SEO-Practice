# Trust and E-E-A-T Recommendations

For this proposed skincare retailer, trust must come from real evidence and dependable operations.

- Publish About Us facts only after the business, team and operating model are confirmed.
- Provide working contact methods and support hours.
- Document supply chain, invoices, batch/expiry handling and authorized status before making authenticity or authorization claims.
- Publish accurate shipping, payment, returns, refund and damaged-order policies; obtain Pakistan-specific legal review.
- Secure checkout and explain privacy/data use accurately.
- For educational content, name actual authors/reviewers and relevant qualifications only with permission and proof; cite reliable primary or authoritative sources; show review dates.
- Collect genuine customer reviews after purchases, disclose incentives, and never fabricate testimonials or ratings.
- Keep product ingredient, usage, size, warnings and availability data aligned with current package/manufacturer sources.
- Avoid diagnosing conditions, guaranteed results, unsupported clinical claims, or implying that cosmetic products treat disease.

No company credentials, dermatologist involvement, certification, reviews, sourcing channel or security feature is currently verified for GlowCare.
