# Navigation and Breadcrumb SEO

## Proposed main navigation
Skincare (Cleansers, Moisturizers, Sunscreens, Serums, Toners, Face Masks) · Hair Care · Body Care · Lip Care · Beauty Accessories · Brands (only stocked brands) · Guides.

Use a manageable menu, clear labels and accessible mobile interactions. Keep search, account and cart controls distinct. Do not expose every brand/category in a crowded top-level nav. Category dropdowns should work by keyboard/touch and retain an accessible focus order.

## Breadcrumb hierarchy
Home > Skincare > Moisturizers > Product name. Guides can use Home > Guides > Topic. Policies should be reachable from footer and relevant checkout steps. Breadcrumbs should reflect actual hierarchy, be clickable except current page, and match canonical page paths.

## Footer
About, contact, shipping, returns, privacy, terms, customer support, and primary category links. Show verified business/contact details only. Avoid duplicating a giant site map.

## SEO and UX value
Consistent navigation and contextual breadcrumbs help visitors understand location and provide crawlable links between parent and child pages. They support discovery but do not guarantee rankings. Verify links and mobile behavior after implementation.
