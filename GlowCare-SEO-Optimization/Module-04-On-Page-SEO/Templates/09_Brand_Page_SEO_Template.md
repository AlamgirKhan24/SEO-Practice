# Brand Page SEO Template

- **URL:** `/brands/{brand}/` only for brands actually stocked and with useful product coverage.
- **Title:** `{Brand} Products in Pakistan | GlowCare` (unique; avoid “official” unless authorized).
- **Meta:** `Explore {Brand} products available at GlowCare. Review verified product details, availability and delivery information.`
- **H1:** `{Brand} products at GlowCare`.
- **Introduction:** Brief factual description using approved brand/company information and verified distribution relationship. Never imply authorization, partnership, origin or authenticity without documentation.
- **Listing:** Current products, filters only where useful, accurate stock/prices and product links.
- **Internal links:** Breadcrumb to Home/Brands, relevant categories, products, and guides.
- **Image SEO:** Use approved brand assets; follow usage permission; descriptive alt for meaningful images.
- **Structured data:** `CollectionPage`/`ItemList` where appropriate; individual product pages carry product structured data. Keep claims and visible content consistent.
- **Keywords:** Brand name + product/category modifier, navigational intent. Use only for brands represented in the real catalog.
