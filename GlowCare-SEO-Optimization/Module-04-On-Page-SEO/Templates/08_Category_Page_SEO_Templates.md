## Suggested sections
H2: Available products; How to compare product information; Related categories; FAQs. Use H3 only for meaningful subcategories. FAQs should reflect real customer questions, not keyword stuffing. Useful questions might cover delivery, product sizes, label information and returns—answer only from confirmed policies.

## Listing and linking
Show accurate price and stock status; allow useful sorting/filtering based on real attributes; avoid indexable thin filter permutations. Link to relevant guides and product pages. Use breadcrumbs and descriptive image alt text. Compress product imagery and ensure mobile filters are usable.

## Keyword alignment
Use the Module 03 primary keyword naturally in title/H1 and relevant copy. Secondary terms should appear only where they accurately describe the category. Category pages target broad shopping intent; guides target learning/comparison intent to reduce cannibalization.
