# Linkable Asset Strategy

Assets are proposals from Module 06, not completed research or published resources. No keyword volume is available.

| Asset | Audience / purpose | Candidate keyword/topic | Potential sources | Promotion | Effort / priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner skincare routine guide | New shoppers; explain label-first, modest routine planning | skincare routine for beginners | Beauty/lifestyle editors, educators, community resource pages | Expert-reviewed article and useful visual summary | Medium / High |
| Cosmetic label-reading guide | All shoppers; explain product fields and limits | how to read skincare ingredients list | Consumer education, universities, publications | Pitch as neutral reference; include source citations | Medium / High |
| Sunscreen label explainer | Sun-care researchers; clarify terms without product rankings | sunscreen labels SPF explained | Health/lifestyle editorial, education resources | Seek reviewer and cite government/dermatology sources | High / High |
| Routine planner checklist | People organizing existing products | beginner skincare routine | Beauty communities, newsletters | Downloadable accessible PDF; no product sales pressure | Medium / Medium |
| Product comparison framework | Shoppers; transparent way to compare labels, size, directions and price | compare skincare products | Consumer editors/resources | Explain criteria and limitations; no unsupported “best” | Medium / Medium |
| Original Pakistan shopper survey | Industry and media; understand shopping questions/trust needs | Pakistan online skincare shopping research | Pakistani publications, universities, industry groups | Publish method, sample limits, anonymized aggregate findings only after real survey | High / Low until research capacity exists |
| Label glossary | Ingredient/label learners | skincare ingredient glossary | Educational blogs, libraries, educators | Maintain citations and update date | Medium / Medium |
| Cosmetic expiry/PAO explainer | Shoppers reading packages | how to check skincare expiry date | Consumer/lifestyle resources | Source-reviewed explainer and infographic | Medium / Medium |
| Product-data transparency standard | Ecommerce buyers; explain product page fields GlowCare verifies | buy skincare online Pakistan | Ecommerce/consumer publications | Publish only after operations implement the standard | High / Medium |
| Category-specific buying checklist | Cleansers/moisturizers/sunscreens | category + buying guide | Relevant beauty editors and resource lists | Create separate checklist only when useful and catalog-supported | Medium / Low |

Do not advertise research or surveys as “original data” before collecting and analyzing data. Do not claim a resource is medically reviewed without actual qualified review.
