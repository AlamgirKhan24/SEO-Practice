# White-Hat Off-Page SEO Strategy

## Objectives
Build brand awareness, relevant referral visits, credible editorial mentions, and a defensible profile of naturally earned links. Off-page work supports discovery and reputation; it cannot guarantee rankings or sales. Because GlowCare is a proposed concept without a live domain, current authority, links or referral data cannot be measured.

## Baseline
Use GSC Links after domain verification; supplement with a reputable backlink index (Ahrefs/Semrush if licensed). Export dated referring pages/domains, destination, anchor, first/last seen, rel attributes, context and referral data. Manually review important sources. Third-party authority/toxicity scores are not proof of value or harm. No metrics or links are claimed in this module.

## Ethical strategies

| Strategy | Fit and steps | Resources / effort | Risks and measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guest contributions | Pitch a genuinely useful, original educational/business article to a publication that accepts contributions; editorial controls anchor/link | Qualified writer/reviewer; 4–8 hours per pitch | Avoid paid followed placements and duplicate articles; measure accepted editorial links/referrals |
| Digital PR | Offer a timely, sourced story or data insight to relevant Pakistan editors | PR lead, source file, fact-check; medium-high | No fabricated trend claims or guaranteed coverage; log mentions, links and referral traffic |
| Original research | Conduct transparent, methodologically sound survey or analysis only after real data collection | Research design, consent/privacy, analyst, reviewer; high | Never present proposals as findings; document method and limitations; measure citations/coverage |
| Educational assets | Promote useful label-reading, sunscreen education and routine resources from Module 06 | Expert review, design, citations; medium | No medical promises; track editorial references and referral use |
| Broken-link outreach | Find a genuinely broken resource link on a relevant site and suggest a close replacement asset if useful | Manual research; low-medium | No scraping/bulk email; the broken link must be checked at contact time |
| Resource-page outreach | Identify curated resource pages where the asset genuinely fits and meets stated criteria | Manual prospect review; medium | No irrelevant directory submissions; record why it fits |
| Unlinked mention | Search for existing accurate GlowCare mentions after launch; politely request a link if it improves reader utility | Monitoring and source verification; low | Do not imply endorsement or demand links; only approach real mentions |
| Supplier/partner links | Ask genuine suppliers/partners to list GlowCare as a retailer where their policy allows and status is documented | Commercial relationship and permissions; low | No reciprocal link deals or false authorization |
| Product review collaboration | Offer transparent review access after legitimate sourcing and real products exist | Product, disclosure and creator agreement; medium-high | No purchase/positive review requirement; disclose compensation and follow platform/ad rules; no followed link demands |
| Community participation | Answer relevant questions transparently as GlowCare, link only when allowed and useful | Staff time; ongoing | No fake identities, spam or self-promotion; measure qualified referrals and helpful engagement |

## Prospect qualification
Score manually for topical relevance, editorial quality, audience fit, legitimacy/transparency, and whether GlowCare’s asset serves their readers. Domain metrics are secondary. Verify live site, editorial/contact policy, ownership, page context, public contact, and link attributes immediately before outreach.

## Competitor gap status
Module 02 did not access paid backlink indexes and recorded competitor backlink metrics unavailable. The workbook provides a comparison procedure, not verified linking domains or gaps. No acquisition opportunity is asserted until source pages are confirmed.
