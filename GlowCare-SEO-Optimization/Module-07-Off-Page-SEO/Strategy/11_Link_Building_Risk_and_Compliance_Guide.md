# Link Building Risk and Compliance Guide

| Risky practice | Why avoid it | Ethical alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Private blog networks/link farms | Artificial placements and low editorial value can violate search spam policies | Earn relevant editorial coverage through useful assets and real relationships |
| Automated bulk link building | Produces irrelevant spam and harms brand reputation | Small, researched, personalized outreach with opt-out respect |
| Spam comments/forum links | Disruptive and rarely useful to readers | Participate helpfully, disclose affiliation, link only when community rules and context allow |
| Irrelevant directories | Low-quality citations and potential paid-link patterns | Use only legitimate, relevant business directories with accurate information |
| Excessive exact-match anchors | Manipulative patterns and poor reader experience | Allow natural, varied editorial anchors; do not request formulas |
| Paid followed links | Payment intended to influence ranking signals is not an ethical tactic | If sponsoring content, disclose and qualify links appropriately (e.g. `rel="sponsored"`) |
| Link exchanges | Scaled reciprocal schemes can manipulate ranking signals | Genuine partner references where independently useful; no quid pro quo |
| Misleading product reviews | Deceives users and may breach advertising rules | Independent opinion, clear sponsorship disclosure, no positive-review requirement |
| Undisclosed sponsorship | Misleads audience and may violate local requirements | Plain disclosure near endorsement and appropriate platform/legal review |
| Fake authors/credentials | Damages trust and misrepresents expertise | Publish verifiable author/reviewer information only |
| Disavow based only on “toxicity score” | Third-party scores are not evidence by themselves | Review manual action and link context; use Google’s disavow guidance only in appropriate cases |

Before any campaign, review current Google Search spam policies, relevant Pakistan advertising/consumer rules, platform policies, and privacy requirements. This guide is not legal advice. Maintain an outreach suppression list and honor requests not to be contacted.
