# Anchor Text Strategy

Use link text chosen by the linking editor or naturally by the source context. GlowCare should not prescribe anchor percentages or require exact-match wording.

- **Branded:** GlowCare, GlowCare guide. Appropriate for citations and brand mentions when accurate.
- **Naked URL:** `glowcare.example` or full URL, often natural in references or social posts.
- **Generic:** “read the guide” or “learn more”; useful in context but less descriptive when alone.
- **Descriptive:** “guide to reading cosmetic labels”; clear to readers and relevant to the destination.
- **Partial match:** A natural phrase containing part of a topic, such as “beginner skincare routine guide.”
- **Exact match:** The exact target phrase. It can occur naturally but should not be solicited or repeated unnaturally.

Monitor anchor context, destination relevance, rel attributes (nofollow, sponsored, ugc), and abrupt patterns. A diverse natural profile is an outcome of genuine editorial choice, not a percentage target. Sponsored links should be properly disclosed and qualified using appropriate attributes. Never pay for a followed link intended to manipulate ranking signals.
