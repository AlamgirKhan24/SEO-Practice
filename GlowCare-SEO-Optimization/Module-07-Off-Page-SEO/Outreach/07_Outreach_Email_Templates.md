# Outreach Email Templates

Templates are drafts for individualized outreach after the site, asset, prospect and contact are verified. No outreach has been sent. Do not use as bulk mail.

## 1. Guest contribution
**Subject:** Article idea for [publication]: [specific reader topic]

Hi [name],

I read [specific relevant article/page] and thought your readers may find a practical guide to [specific topic] useful. I’m [real name/role] with GlowCare, a proposed skincare ecommerce project in Pakistan. We are preparing an evidence-reviewed piece on [angle], with sources and a clear non-medical scope.

Would you consider an original contribution for [section]? I can send a short outline first. Any links would be editorially selected and clearly relevant.

Best,
[real name]
[verified contact / site]

## 2. Resource page inclusion
**Subject:** Resource suggestion for [resource page]

Hello [name],

Your [resource page title] includes [specific relevant resources]. If useful to your audience, our [specific guide URL] explains [reader benefit] and cites [source types]. It is currently [published/draft—use actual status].

Would it meet your inclusion criteria? Please feel free to decline if it is not a fit.

Regards,
[real name]

## 3. Broken link
**Subject:** Possible broken link on [page title]

Hi [name],

While using your [page URL], I noticed the link labeled “[label]” appears to return [verified error]. I thought I’d flag it in case helpful. If you are updating the page, [GlowCare asset URL] may be a relevant alternative because [specific topical match]; no need to use it if it does not meet your standards.

Thanks,
[real name]

## 4. Digital PR pitch
**Subject:** Pakistan skincare ecommerce story idea: [verified finding]

Would this be relevant to [section/readers]? I can provide the underlying methodology and answer questions. This is not a product efficacy claim.

## 5. Product review collaboration

Product details and sourcing documentation can be shared for review: [only if available].

Would you like the collaboration terms to review?

[real name]

## 6. Educational resource promotion

Regards,
[real name]

## 7. Unlinked brand mention
**Subject:** Thank you for mentioning GlowCare

Hi [name],

Thank you for mentioning GlowCare on [page URL]. I’m [real name/role]. If you think readers would benefit, would you consider linking the brand name to [most relevant destination]? This is optional; the reference is appreciated either way. Please let me know if the mention is inaccurate and I can provide verified details.

Best,
[real name]

**Disclosure:** Clearly identify paid/sponsored arrangements and use appropriate link qualification. Do not disguise compensation or claim prior relationships that do not exist.
