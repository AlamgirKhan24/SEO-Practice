# Module 3: Keyword Research

## Project context

- **Practice business:** GlowCare Salon (fictional)
- **Target area:** University Town, Peshawar, Pakistan
- **Website URL:** Not built yet; page URLs below are proposed slugs.
- **Research status:** This is a starter keyword set based on the salon's assumed services and a preliminary web search. Search volume and SEO difficulty are intentionally left for verification in Google Keyword Planner or another keyword tool; no volume figures have been invented.

## How to use this module

Search for the phrases below in Google Keyword Planner with location set to Peshawar (and the language your customers use). Expand them with Autocomplete, related searches, and questions. Record the date, location, language, tool, volume range, and difficulty where available. Keep a phrase only if it matches a service GlowCare actually offers. The current service assumptions are haircuts, hair coloring, bridal makeup, and skincare.

## Initial content gaps

- Separate service pages for hair services, coloring, bridal makeup, party makeup, and skincare.
- A clear services and pricing page, if the business can publish accurate prices or starting prices.
- A bridal makeup guide answering common planning questions.
- A contact page with consistent address, phone, hours, map, and appointment instructions.
- Actual photos, service details, and business information are needed before publishing; do not claim fictional services as real business facts.
