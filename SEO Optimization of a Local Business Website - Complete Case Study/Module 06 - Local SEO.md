# Module 6: Local SEO

## Project context

- **Practice business:** GlowCare Salon (fictional)
- **Target area:** University Town, Peshawar, Pakistan
- **Status:** This module is a plan for a hypothetical business. Do not create a Google Business Profile, directory listings, reviews, or business schema using invented details. Complete those steps only for a real, eligible business with the owner's authorization and verified information.

## What to do

Local SEO helps a real local business appear for relevant nearby searches. Google's published guidance says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and prominence. Accurate information can help Google match a profile to relevant searches, but no checklist guarantees a particular ranking.

## Checklist for a real business

- [ ] Confirm the business meets Google's eligibility rules and that you are authorized by the owner to manage its profile.
- [ ] Claim or create one profile for the real location. Complete Google's verification process. A fictional business or virtual-only address is not eligible.
- [ ] Use the real-world business name and the most accurate primary category. Add only categories and services that genuinely describe the business; do not add keywords to the name.
- [ ] Enter the verified address or service area, phone, website, hours, and service details. Keep them accurate and consistent with the business's actual operations.
- [ ] Add genuine, current photos and videos of the business, staff, and work, with appropriate permission.
- [ ] Keep the same business name, address, and phone (NAP) on the website and relevant directory listings. Do not publish private addresses or inaccurate details.
- [ ] Add the verified contact details and directions to the website. Embed a map only where helpful and ensure the map points to the correct real location.
- [ ] Create a location page only for a real location or genuinely served area with useful, distinct information. Avoid thin pages made only to target many neighborhood names.
- [ ] Ask customers for honest reviews after genuine service, without incentives, pressure, or requests for specific wording. Respond professionally to reviews.
- [ ] Share useful, accurate updates on the profile when there is news, an offer, or an event. A weekly posting schedule is optional; do not treat it as a ranking requirement.
- [ ] Review profile features such as messaging or Q&A in the account. Availability can vary; do not promise features that are not available.
- [ ] Add citations only to relevant, reputable platforms that serve the market. Record the listing URL and check details periodically.
- [ ] Keep any LocalBusiness structured data aligned with the real details displayed on the website.
- [ ] Record baseline and end-of-project profile metrics over comparable periods, where available.

## What to record for the final report

- Evidence of the real, verified profile's information, with sensitive details handled appropriately.
- Comparable profile performance data at the start and end, including date ranges and metric definitions.
- Review count and average rating at the start and end; do not imply reviews were caused by SEO without evidence.
- Citation tracker with listing URLs, consistency checks, and dates.
- Dated local visibility checks with exact query and search location.
- Screenshots of before-and-after profile details and any local landing page changes.
- Any metrics that were unavailable, and why.
